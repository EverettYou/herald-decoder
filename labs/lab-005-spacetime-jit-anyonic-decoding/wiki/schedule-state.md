commute globally. A compact positive-phase orbit from `|0^108>` establishes
one nonzero common star/triangle `+1` state without enumerating its enormous
support or choosing an absolute logical sector. This does not supply a
sequential measurement law, correction-conditioned second public record, or
noisy repeated-history kernel. Derive and validate those on the same fixed
state before any performance sampling.

The general operator-derived transition and noisy five-round integration

The [J6Q alternate-path exact matrix](records/j6q-alternate-path-operator-law-matrix-2026-09-25.md)

deterministic vacuum charge in every tested branch. Both conditional laws
are independent of the first result where it varies. This establishes a
geometry contrast for ideal two-edge faults, not a general D4 transition

The [three-edge chain/star matrix](records/j6r-three-edge-topology-causal-law-2026-09-25.md)
now tests a longer connected path against a branched-tree control. On the

second record whose site-2 charge repeats the first site-2 charge; two-edge
correction instead has an identical complete second law across first results.
The matched chain requires a four-random-site calculation and is censored at
the registered three-site cap. The star has a deterministic first record.
This is an exact causal-record effect, plausibly a repeat-stabilizer effect,
not yet the sought hidden-charge feedback or a noisy schedule result.

The [separately registered repeat and four-site control](records/j6s-repeat-transfer-four-site-2026-09-25.md)

one-edge correction, site 2 is the *same operator* before and after; the
newly eligible site 0 is `1/2` independent of all first records. Under
matched correction, four second sites are individually random but admit
only four correlated strings (`0000`, `1010`, `0101`, `1111`), each `1/4`
under every first record. This is ideal-state evidence of repetition and
joint correlation, not new-site first-record transfer, a general noisy

frozen.

The [four remaining action-subset exact test](records/j6t-alternate-action-new-site-law-2026-09-25.md)

second record depend on the first, but through another unchanged repeated

vacuous for the transfer test. The joint changed-plus-new site projections
