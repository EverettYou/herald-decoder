## Summary

Both tied five-edge alternative initial errors retain a positive, identical
complete first record, but neither fits the inherited full-second-law exact
engine budget once its actual second-variable-site counts are known. This is
an implementation-cost gate, not a proof that the second laws contain no
private-error information.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j7u-conditional-second-site-count-preflight-2026-09-27.json)
pins the prior peer selection, periodic D4 orbit, complete first public
record, two relation-free corrections, source implementations, and a strict
46,000-term/60-CPU-second preflight. The [machine result](../../results/j7u-conditional-second-site-count-preflight-2026-09-27.json)
passes all eleven pins before and after. Both peers have exact selected
first-record mass `1/16`. Across four action/error cells, all 88 eligible
one-site second marginals were evaluated in exactly 45,088 ordered terms;
there were no complete second joint laws, histories, arms, or bootstraps.

For each error, action `[0]` has one non-deterministic second site (`0`),
while action `[1,30,32,34,35]` has five (`0,17,20,21,22`). The frozen
two-action `full_law` implementation therefore projects 285,744 ordered
terms for either peer, above its inherited 65,536-term ceiling. These
equal *counts* do not imply equal second joint laws: unmeasured correlations
may distinguish the private errors.

## Status

This four-cell prerequisite is closed. No peer is promoted to a full-law
run at the current engine ceiling, and the ceiling is not raised. A
separately preregistered algebraic joint-law reduction could be checked
next using the existing exact marginals and operator relations; any
information claim still requires normalized *complete* second public laws
for both initial errors under matched actions and the common first record.
Full-channel paired logical risk remains an independent denominator and
scoring problem.
