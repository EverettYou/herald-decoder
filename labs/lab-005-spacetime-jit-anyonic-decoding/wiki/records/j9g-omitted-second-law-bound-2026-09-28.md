## Summary

At the one selected ideal L=2 complete first public record, all 55 private errors with computed second public laws have exactly the same full flux/charge/vacuum law under each of two separately fixed public actions. The remaining 8,137 errors have exact posterior weight `0.61552445%` from the complete first-record census. Whatever their still-unknown valid second laws are, they can move the full conditional second-law distribution by at most that total-variation amount from the known common law under either action. This is a worst-case finite-record certificate, not the actual second-law mixture or a full-channel result.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j9g-omitted-second-law-bound-2026-09-28.json) freezes the J9F denominator, all 8,192 exact prior-weighted first masses, all 110 J8I complete second laws, and actions `[0]` and `[1,30,32,34,35]`. The [machine certificate](../../results/j9g-omitted-second-law-bound-2026-09-28.json) recomputes the first-record total and known-error share from all masks and replays every normalized full-public-array law. The 55 laws have two positive rows under the singleton action and 32 under the five-edge action; within each action they agree exactly. No new Born term, complete law, history, schedule arm or bootstrap was produced; the certificate took `0.092578` CPU seconds.

Let `ε=809314423250142987013913/131483716067635033072123673 ≈ 0.0061552445`. Under either fixed action, the actual but uncomputed full mixture must be `M=(1−ε)Q+εR`, where `Q` is that action's verified common law and `R` is some valid mixture of the omitted errors' laws. Thus `TV(M,Q)≤ε`, and any event probability differs from its `Q` value by at most `ε`. This does **not** bound a posterior conditional on a rare individual second outcome, which could be dominated by omitted errors.

For a fixed hidden target and an observation-independent 0–1 loss, the maximum Bayes-risk *improvement* from seeing the second record at this first record and fixed action is also at most `ε` (0.6155 percentage points). This follows because the second record cannot distinguish among the 55 known errors, while the omitted class has total weight `ε`. A separate conservative bound for information about the private error is `h₂(ε)+ε log₂(8137) = 0.134015031978` bits. Neither number is a measured gain, and neither applies to adaptive JIT action selection or the program's unconditional Boolean-union schedule score.

## Status

The registered two-action worst-case certificate passes. It narrows one selected first record despite 8,137 missing second laws. Other first records can behave differently: the already verified finite positive collision witness remains a separate evidence branch. A next bounded transition should weight that positive witness by its absolute first-record probability and seek a defensible physical-prior information lower bound before any full-IID or logical-risk claim. No omitted law may be filled by symmetry or by this bound.
