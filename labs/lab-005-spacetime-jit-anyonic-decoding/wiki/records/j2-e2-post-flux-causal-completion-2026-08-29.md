# J2 E2 causal post-flux completion

## Result

The bounded deterministic paper-\(L=2\) interface now answers the registered E2 question positively. A causal stage-1 D4 flux action can be followed by an action-bound public charge/vacuum report, a public charge-recovery action, and—on causal schedules—a later prefix attached only after charge completion. Both public modes complete all four registered schedule branches. This is interface-correctness evidence, not evidence of decoder or schedule performance.

## Necessary registration correction

The pre-execution fixture falsified one wording-level invariant. Syndrome-only and heralded choose different stage-1 flux actions in every branch, so a physical measurement performed afterward cannot be forced to be the same action-independent record. The corrected matched design holds the pre-action history, physical measurement layout, and report rule fixed, while conditioning and binding each post-action report to its mode's actual intervention. This restores causal semantics without changing the scientific question, matrix, compute budget, or acceptance endpoint.

## Two-stage public interface

E2A accepts only a typed charge/vacuum report bound to the exact flux-action digest and combines it with an explicitly supplied physical layout. It does not receive projector truth, physical faults, future observations, logical labels, or private scoring. The perfect fixture reproduces the existing E0 post-flux observation and charge action. Wrong action binding, temporal inversion, illegal labels, simultaneous labels, and layout mismatch fail closed.

E2B/E2C then exercise the complete matrix:

| Public mode | Immediate | Fixed delay 1 | JIT | Offline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Syndrome only | causal, complete | causal, complete | causal, complete | noncausal, complete |
| Heralded | causal, complete | causal, complete | causal, complete | noncausal, complete |

All eight cells return a nonempty blue charge correction. The causal branches expose a next prefix only after completion; offline consumes full history, remains explicitly noncausal, and makes no next-prefix claim. Future-only changes preserve all six causal cells while changing the two offline cells. Private-truth-only changes preserve all eight public outputs, replay is bit identical, and parity-inconsistent charge support fails closed.
