## Summary

The smallest selected first-record/next-site screen finds a nonzero ideal
measurement-versus-no-measurement influence on four of five fixed geometries.
It does **not** find a matched-history action contrast: the already complete
two-edge comparison remains exactly null after both branches measure their
respective second records.

## Evidence

The [preregistered contract](../../manifests/j7f-state-sensitive-influence-screen-2026-09-25.json)
fixes one structurally open branch per previously screened topology, one
canonical positive complete first public record, the first cross-round
anticommuting star pair, source pins and a 60-CPU-second/262,144-term ceiling.
The [exact result](../../results/j7f-state-sensitive-influence-screen-2026-09-25.json)
passes all six pins before and after, all five actual-sector/first-mass gates,
the two-edge explicit sum over both outcomes of the anticommuting second
projector, and the independent two-edge complete-law null control. It used
8,512 ordered moment terms and no stochastic work or caller mutation.

| Frozen geometry | Selected first-record mass | Next-star mean if second block is not measured | Mean after all actual-sector second outcomes are marginalized |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Two-edge path | 1/2 | 1 | 0 |
| Three-edge chain | 1/4 | 1 | 0 |
| Four-edge path | 1/8 | 1 | 0 |
| Four-edge branch | 1/2 | 1 | 0 |
| Six-edge loop | 1/16 | 0 | 0 |

The complete second block is used in the algebra: a commuting projective
block nonselectively dephases a later star to zero if any member
anticommutes with it on the triangle-vacuum orbit. On the two-edge control,
an explicit chronological sum over the selected anticommuting star's two
outcomes gives joint `P(first,+next)=1/4`, or conditional `P(+next|first)=1/2`,
matching the dephasing identity. Its unmeasured-second mean is `1`.

## Status

This is a state-sensitive *one-site* influence gate against an artificial
unmeasured-second counterfactual. It does not establish that two allowed
public correction/future-fault histories have different complete next-first
records; indeed the two-edge full-law result has TV `0` for both matched
first records. Other first records, correlations among next sites, alternative
actions, larger topologies, the full physical D4 density channel and noisy
five-round schedule effects remain open. The next discriminating comparison
must match final physical support and first record and calculate both full
public laws, rather than promoting this gate as a feedback result.
