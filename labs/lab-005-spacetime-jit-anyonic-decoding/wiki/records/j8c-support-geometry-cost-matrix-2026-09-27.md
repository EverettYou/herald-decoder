## Summary

On the frozen paper-normalized L=2 D4 graph, the shortest untested graph-simple cycle length is eight: 54 cycles, all nontrivial windings. Toggling each onto the base private red-X error preserves the complete first public flux, and every resulting error has positive exact mass for the selected all-zero first charge record. These are new matched-record private-error hypotheses in distinct winding classes, not yet a second-record or logical-risk result. Paper L=3 has only a source graph in this check; the required operator orbit and sequential public instrument are not pinned there.

## Evidence

The [prospective matrix](../../manifests/j8c-support-geometry-cost-matrix-2026-09-27.json) covers all graph-simple four- and eight-edge cycles on the frozen L=2 topology, including trivial and nontrivial homology, with an exact replay of all twelve earlier six-edge trivial cycles. It conditionally authorizes an all-candidate first-mass calculation only if the complete matrix fits ten variable sites and 65,536 ordered first-projector terms. A separate L=3 branch checks topology/readiness only, never a transferred L=2 measurement law.

The [machine result](../../results/j8c-support-geometry-cost-matrix-2026-09-27.json) finds no four-edge simple cycles and 54 eight-edge simple cycles. The 54 split equally among winding vectors `(1,0)`, `(0,1)` and `(1,-1)`; none is homologically trivial. All preserve the base first flux. Exact selected-first masses are positive for all 54: four `1/16`, four `1/64` and 46 `1/256`. The full calculation used 28,992 ordered first-projector terms, below the frozen cap, with at most ten variable sites. Candidate red-X weights range from five to eleven. The L=3 source graph has 54 vertices and 81 red edges, but no matched L=3 J6L/J6M operator-orbit and sequential-instrument evidence was produced. All pins and six-cycle replay pass. No second public law, history, schedule arm or bootstrap ran.

## Status

The L=2 winding-distinct alternatives are in-scope candidates for a prospectively costed conditional second-public-law test with the *same* public first record and relation-free actions. Their homology changes the private error class; it does not by itself show an observable logical-risk improvement or even that the second public record distinguishes them. All 54 supports were enumerated; any later submatrix must set a pre-data cost bound and cover each winding class rather than selecting a favorable outcome. L=3 remains behind fresh physical-state and public-instrument derivation; it is not a direct comparison to these L=2 laws. Full-IID information, unconditional logical loss, noisy JIT and thresholds remain open.
