## Summary

Both preregistered exact local projector branches pass on the fixed periodic L=2 vacuum orbit. The six-edge loop has six individually random first eligible sites, exceeding the frozen four-site full-public cap, so no complete 24-site loop record is claimed. A separately fixed three-block sequence propagates selected-star postselection through two corrections and yields a normalized third local law. Neither branch is a general D4 state-feedback kernel or noisy JIT result.

## Evidence

The [contract](../../manifests/j6w-loop-and-three-block-ideal-projector-matrix-2026-09-25.json) pins the primary-source PDF, L=2 incidence/orbit, six-edge loop witness and unchanged five-round caller. The [machine result](../../results/j6w-loop-and-three-block-ideal-projector-matrix-2026-09-25.json) passes all five hashes and exact rational nonnegative/normalization checks.

In the loop branch, the frozen site rule selects first sites 0 and 1, both half/half, from six variable eligible sites. Correcting edge 0 selects second sites 17 and 20: their four local outcomes each have conditional mass `1/4` for every selected first outcome. Matched correction has no second site with nonzero unconditional variance under the registered rule, so its selected second block is empty; this is vacuous, not a vacuum full-record claim. Crucially, only selected stars were measured in this local instrument. Unselected-star measurement/dephasing was *not* included, so these probabilities must not be treated as marginals of a complete public readout.

In the independent three-block fixture, the physical pair is red edges 0 and 4. After first green-site-1 measurement and correction of edge 0, second blue-site-0 has four positive first/second prefixes, each of joint mass `1/4`. If no second correction occurs, a third measurement of the same blue-site-0 operator repeats its second result with probability one. If edge 4 is then corrected, a third local measurement of blue sites 0 and 2 gives the equal-bit pair matching the second bit with probability one under every prefix. This is an exact selected-projector postselection identity for one ideal orbit; it does not establish a complete third public record, hidden-charge intervention benefit, general action-conditioned density channel, or five-round noisy schedule risk.

## Status

Both local branches pass within the registered 60-CPU-second cap; no histories, schedule arms or bootstraps were generated. Full loop public law remains censored at six variable first sites versus cap four. Next discriminating gate is a separately registered full-record/dephasing treatment under an explicit compute cap and a matched action-feedback-versus-repeat test that can isolate a genuinely new-site third-record effect. No existing noisy schedule result is promoted.
