# J1 E3 repeated D4 transition-kernel completeness

## Result

The registered audit returns a verified localized negative. The current
source-backed and project-defined components do **not** jointly specify

\[
P(X_{t+1},O_{t+1}\mid X_t,F_t,A_t).
\]

K1 physical-state evolution and K3 action-conditioned physical post-flux
generation are missing. K2 supplies normalized local classical report channels
only after truth is given. K4 privately scores one completed spatial cycle but
does not yet define the accumulated spacetime residual. No stochastic history
was generated.

This prevents a scientifically arbitrary step: independently resampling a
single-round fusion record would erase whatever hidden projector or residual
state is needed to determine the next round. The audit therefore localizes the
missing physics before decoder or schedule performance is measured.

## Factor analysis

| Factor | Status | What is supported | What remains missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 physical-state evolution | missing | Static observation conditional on a supplied physical chain; deterministic digest update for supplied true labels | A normalized next-state law conditional on previous hidden state, data faults, and applied correction |
| K2 public measurement | localized-only | Exact normalized E1 local report rows, normalized categorical confusion rows, typed reports, perfect-report limit | Joint multisite and cross-round distribution of true D4 outcomes |
| K3 action-conditioned post-flux | missing | Exact E2 action binding and order when a post-action report is externally supplied | Physical residual-state update under the flux action and a normalized charge/vacuum report generated from that state |
| K4 private logical scorer | partial | Ground-state-relative homology score for one completed spatial chain and correction | Canonical accumulated spacetime residual and justification of the final logical criterion |

The earliest missing dependency is K1. K3 depends on it: a physically generated
post-flux observation cannot be defined before the correction-updated state is
defined. Normalized classical noise rows in K2 do not generate the missing true
state, and the K4 score cannot repair an undefined preceding transition.

## Why a bounded experiment cannot choose the model

The source audit and exact checks leave three materially different closures:

1. An explicit phenomenological Markov transition on a declared finite hidden
   D4 state. This is the smallest implementable extension, but it adds a new
   physical assumption rather than reproducing a transition supplied by the
   sources.
2. A stateful projector or density-operator evolution. This is closest to the
   projector semantics and can retain noncommuting memory, but it requires a
   substantially larger mathematical state definition.
3. An ancilla- or circuit-derived repeated extraction channel. This gives the
   strongest operational account of measurement faults, but requires circuit
   primitives and dependencies that the audited D4 sources do not presently
   provide.

Each can be normalized and made to pass the present tiny exact interface tests.
Those tests therefore cannot discriminate among them. Choosing one changes the
physical model, dependency and compute budget, and scientific meaning of a
future fault-tolerance result. This is a genuine researcher decision rather
than an ordering choice among cheap reversible diagnostics.

## Verification and claim boundary

- Six of six focused K1--K4 tests pass.
- All 137 current Lab 005 tests pass.
- The exact E1 and generic categorical report rows sum to one.
- Wiki lint has zero findings; all 12 knowledge-graph and four document-viewer
  tests pass.
- Zero Monte Carlo samples were taken.

The result establishes only the completeness gap. It does not establish a noisy
D4 generator, logical-error rate, schedule ranking, threshold, hardware
fidelity, or fault tolerance.
