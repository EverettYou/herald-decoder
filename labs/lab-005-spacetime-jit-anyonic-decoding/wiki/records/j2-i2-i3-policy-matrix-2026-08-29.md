# J2 I2/I3 typed E1 policy matrix

I2 and I3 are verified for the registered deterministic paper-\(L=2\) scope. One immutable four-row public trace was sent through both public D4 stage-1 modes under immediate, one-round fixed delay, bounded JIT, and explicitly noncausal offline scheduling. All eight cells invoked the actual public policy and returned corrections whose boundary reproduces the supplied two-vertex flux syndrome.

The two modes share history, cluster timing, scheduler requests, and decoder implementation. `syndrome_only` receives no charge measurements; `heralded` receives all 24 public charge-membership bits. A site carrying both `m_flux` and `e_charge` reaches heralded mode without coercion.

Fourteen focused tests and all 121 Lab 005 tests pass. This is stage-1 interface evidence only.
