# J1 E1 typed-policy translation registration

The E1 execution localized a typed multi-label record that the first restricted adapter rejected. This follow-up asks whether the record is actually outside the existing D4 policy schema or merely outside that adapter's implementation.

The smallest test is a three-cell deterministic matrix. T1A projects the public record into the declared lower-information `syndrome_only` mode: it preserves flux membership and withholds charge membership. T1B factorizes the same record losslessly for `heralded`: flux membership enters the syndrome field, while charge membership becomes an explicit measured 0/1 outcome. A site reporting both labels occupies both fields; the adapter never guesses one latent true label. T1C checks digest binding, replay, truth exclusion, and the old rejection as a negative control.

All cells use the existing paper-L=2 interface and actual public policy entry point. Their combined deterministic runtime is capped below five CPU minutes and 1 GiB. No stochastic sample, schedule comparison, LER, threshold, or fault-tolerance claim is permitted. A schema extension is considered only if a concrete E1 public record cannot preserve its mode-available bits and provenance through the current fields.
