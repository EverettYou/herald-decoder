## Summary

The first three-edge extension finds an actual dependence of the complete
second public record on the first public record for one specified correction
prefix. In the connected chain, two eligible first stars each have a binary
charge result. After correcting only its first edge, the second blue:2 charge
repeats the observed first blue:2 bit, while a different eligible site stays
half/half. This is a causal public-record dependence, but it is consistent
with repeat measurement of an unchanged observable—not yet evidence of a
non-Abelian hidden-state feedback mechanism. The matched chain branch is
explicitly censored because four second sites are nondeterministic, beyond
the preregistered three-site exact-support cap.

defer, one-edge prefix, two-edge prefix and matched correction. All eight
branches pass flux/eligibility and same-sector projector-commutation gates.
Seven have complete normalized exact first/second public laws. The eighth
retains only diagnostic random-site counts; it publishes no partial public
joint law.

For the chain one-edge correction, the exact second public support differs
between first records with site-2 charge 0 and 1; the conditional site-0 bit
is `1/2` in both. The comparison uses all three 24-bit public arrays and
exact rational support, not a private state or one marginal. In the
two-edge correction, the complete conditional second distribution is the
same for all four positive first records. The star has only one first record,
so first-outcome dependence is not testable there. Defer has no E2.

The newly observed dependence is narrow: blue:2 is measured both before and
after the one-edge action and remains eligible. A separate structural check
is needed before attributing the dependence to hidden non-Abelian charge
transfer. The matched-chain four-site law must be separately preregistered
at a raised support bound if needed. The old five-round caller is unchanged;
176 Lab 005 tests pass. No stochastic histories, schedule arms or bootstrap
replicates were produced.

The later [separate four-site exact control](j6s-repeat-transfer-four-site-2026-09-25.md)
closes the matched-chain calculation and verifies the unchanged site-2
operator under one-edge correction. It does not retroactively remove this
record's three-site registered censor.

## Status

Seven exact branches pass and one is censored by the registered support cap.
This finite ideal-state fixture is not a general action-conditioned D4
transition, noisy measurement model, or JIT schedule-risk result. The next
bounded prerequisite is to distinguish unchanged-observable repetition from
new-site transfer and, separately, resolve the matched-chain four-site joint
law under a new explicit budget before any physical five-round integration.
