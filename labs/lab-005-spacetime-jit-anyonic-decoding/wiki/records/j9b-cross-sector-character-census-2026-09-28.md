## Summary

The exact periodic D4 orbit character calculation replays all 48 complete second-public laws in the frozen 12-sector, two-action deterministic-first collision class. Each law is uniform on the affine bit set selected by its deterministic signed parity constraints. The class has 18 null and six positive pairwise contrasts; in this class alone, parity-rank pairs separate TV `0`, `1/2` and `3/4` without a rank-pair conflict.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j9b-cross-sector-character-census-2026-09-28.json) freezes every J8X sector and both public actions, including all null controls. The [machine result](../../results/j9b-cross-sector-character-census-2026-09-28.json) checks 424 exact active-site parity products against the stored direct public-law Walsh moments. It derives an independent signed GF(2) constraint basis for each law, reconstructs the exact uniform affine support, and replays each complete pairwise TV. The maximum active site set has six charges; all outside sites are fixed identically within each pair. First complete records are deterministic and action/flux, commutation, involution, normalization, source-hash and no-new-Born gates pass in `0.060064` CPU seconds.

The 24 pair/action cells divide into 18 TV-zero, two TV-`1/2` and four TV-`3/4` cells. Every TV-zero cell has parity-rank pair `(0,0)`; both TV-`1/2` cells have `(1,0)`; the TV-`3/4` cells have `(2,0)` or `(0,2)`. Five positive cells first differ at one public charge bit. Only the J9A five-edge-action cell requires a three-site parity; it replays the two disjoint triple constraints and TV `3/4`. These are observed regularities of the *evaluated class*, not a proof that rank alone determines laws elsewhere.

## Status

The registered class census is closed. Signed parity constraints and the orbit algebra reproduce the already-computed finite class exactly; rank-pair TV pooling is empirically consistent within those 24 cells but is not a validated operator automorphism, a way to skip Born-law evaluation for new sectors, or a full-IID information/risk result. Other first records, the remaining first-flux sectors and errors, and any noisy spacetime channel remain unmeasured. A further reduction would need an explicit transformation that preserves the public action, signed operator characters and record law, then direct held-out-law validation.
