## Summary

On one selected positive first record of a fixed ideal three-edge D4
geometry, two fully measured histories with the same initial and final
red-X support have **different complete next-first public Born laws**.
Their exact total variation is `1/2`. This is finite ideal-history
sensitivity, not an isolated causal effect of the public correction:
the branches have different later physical-fault keys.

## Evidence

The [preregistered contract](../../manifests/j7g-three-edge-matched-complete-next-law-2026-09-25.json)
fixes the J6M periodic common vacuum orbit and private initial red-X edges
`[0,4,3]`. It reuses the J7F positive complete first public record of
probability `1/4`: charge zero at the two variable first sites `1,2`.
Branch A corrects edge `0`, measures the full actual-residual-sector second
record, then has a private later fault on edge `0`. Branch B corrects edge
`4`, measures its own full second record, then has a private later fault on
edge `4`. Both finish on support `[0,4,3]`.

The [machine result](../../results/j7g-three-edge-matched-complete-next-law-2026-09-25.json)
has two positive second prefixes in A and one in B. Every complete 24-site
binary second and next-first public law normalizes. In A, next-first site
`1` is charged with probability `1/2`; in B it is vacuum with probability
`1`. In this selected record all other next public bits coincide across
the marginalized branches, so complete-record total variation is `1/2`.
There are three positive second rows, five next rows and 8,972 exact ordered
moment terms. Eight source/caller pins pass before and after; five binding
negative controls, deterministic completion replay and two focused tests
pass. The earlier J7C three-edge and J7E two-edge exact nulls are retained
separately and never pooled.

## Status

This resolves one finite ideal **history** discriminator. It does not hold
the later fault fixed while changing only the action, cover all first
records, derive a general noisy D4 density-state transition, integrate the
five-round caller, estimate schedule risk, or identify a threshold. No
stochastic histories, arm evaluations or bootstraps were generated. The
next scientific gate is a same-future-exogenous-key action contrast or a
clear statement of why the current matched-support result cannot be
promoted into a causal action-conditioned kernel.
