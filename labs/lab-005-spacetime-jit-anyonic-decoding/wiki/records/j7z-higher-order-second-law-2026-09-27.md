## Summary

The remaining higher-order charge correlations also vanish in all
twelve same-first-record private-error alternatives. Together with
the pinned lower-order moments, this gives the exact *complete*
second public law for each alternative under both fixed actions.
Every candidate law equals the base law on this selected ideal
periodic fixture. This is a finite thirteen-support, selected-record
null, not a full physical-IID-channel or logical-risk theorem.

7,944 orbit-moment terms were used. Joining the earlier 208 zero
singleton/pair moments gives all 31 nontrivial parity moments of the
five variable second charges. Walsh inversion yields exactly 32
equiprobable complete second public records under action
`[1,30,32,34,35]`; action `[0]` has two equiprobable records.
These are the same *record keys and probabilities* for every one
of the thirteen errors. The base laws replay the prior complete
result exactly, and the two five-edge peer laws independently
replay their prior complete results. There were zero ordered
projector expansions, histories, schedule arms or bootstraps.

## Status

The two-action second public record has identical error-conditioned
likelihood for all thirteen tested errors *given this selected first
record*. Thus it cannot distinguish errors within a prior restricted
to this thirteen-support set under either fixed action. The result
does not cover other physical errors in the 36-edge IID channel,
other first records, larger geometry, logical-sector/Boolean-union
loss, a policy-emitted action or noisy repeated measurement. The
next in-scope prerequisite is a separately registered alternative
first-record/error/geometry discriminator, with any resource change
explicitly bounded before computation.
