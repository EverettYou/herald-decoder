## Summary

Two preregistered, no-new-fault exact branches pass. A private postselected
projector token can bind a complete first public record, correction action,
and complete second public record to the next first observation on one fixed
two-edge path. The next record repeats the first after defer and the second
after either correction. A separate complete six-edge-loop instrument repeats
the second record at the next first observation for all 16 positive prefixes.
These are ideal measurement limits, not a general noisy feedback channel.

## Evidence

The [contract](../../manifests/j7a-postselected-next-first-limiting-fixtures-2026-09-25.json)
pins the source PDF, fixed periodic graph, prepared vacuum orbit, two earlier
public laws, and unchanged five-round caller. The [machine result](../../results/j7a-postselected-next-first-limiting-fixtures-2026-09-25.json)
passes all six pins, 10 positive two-edge-path cases (2 defer, 4 partial,
4 matched), and 16 positive loop first/second prefixes within the 60-CPU-second
cap. Ordered exact projector contractions give conditional next-first mass one
on the repeated complete 24-site `flux/charge/vacuum` record in every case.
For the loop, an explicit three-block contraction checks site 17, whose second
bit varies between prefixes. On the path, all seven registered swapped-action,
tampered-record, stale-prefix, unsupported-action, private-field-injection,
and cross-trial controls reject. There are zero stochastic histories, schedule
arm evaluations, and bootstrap replicates. Exact replay passes.

The prototype keeps the postselected projector list and physical mask in a
private state token; its public completion contains only prefix/action
digests and the next full binary record. The no-new-fault completion also
rejects any changed next public bit. This token is not used by the frozen
five-round caller.

## Status

Current as a bounded ideal repeat and interface-integrity control. Repeating
the preceding record when no new physical fault or action occurs is projector
idempotence, not evidence for hidden-charge feedback under future faults.

risk, scaling and threshold remain unvalidated. The next gate must separately
freeze a nonzero future-fault action-conditioned next-first law and caller
