## Summary

An exact structural screen finds finite cross-round noncommutation openings
inside already validated D4 geometries, including the smallest two-edge path.
This does **not** establish a different next-first public distribution: the
earlier three-edge exact null itself has such an opening. It only prioritizes
one separately registered Born-law test over a larger topology or five-round
caller integration.

## Evidence

The [preregistered contract](../../manifests/j7d-cross-round-commutation-screen-2026-09-25.json)
fixes five existing source-validated supports, every nonempty in-support
correction, zero/one future fault from the same support, complete eligible
second and next-first star sets, matched initial/final support, and strict
structural-compute caps. The [machine result](../../results/j7d-cross-round-commutation-screen-2026-09-25.json)
passes all eight before/after source pins, 23,381 same-sector commuting-pair
controls, 281,960 cross-round operator checks, and J7C branch coverage.

The first ranked structural candidate compares correction of edge 0 followed
by future physical fault 0 with correction of edge 4 followed by future
physical fault 4, both on the original two-edge support [0,4]. Each branch
has one eligible second/next anticommuting star pair. Different second
measurement sectors and fault timing still prevent an isolated action-effect
interpretation. The three-edge J7C pair also has a structural opening in one
branch, yet its exact complete-law total variation was zero at all four first
records. Thus noncommutation is necessary for this dephasing route but plainly
not sufficient for an observable difference.

The scan covers 628 histories and 1,624 support-matched pairs in under the
registered caps; no exact Born-law enumeration, new dependency, stochastic
history, schedule arm, bootstrap, or frozen-caller invocation occurred.

## Status

Closed as a finite necessary-condition screen, not a physical noisy D4
feedback kernel or a general no-go. The next bounded test can preregister the
two-edge structural candidate's complete action-conditioned second records
and exact next-first Born laws, with the J7C null as a control. No schedule
performance or threshold inference is licensed.
