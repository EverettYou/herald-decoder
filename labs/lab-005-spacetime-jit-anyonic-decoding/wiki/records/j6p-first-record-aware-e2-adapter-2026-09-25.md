## Summary

The J6P adapter turns the exact J6O public law into a causally bound second
record for one ideal periodic L=2 D4 path. Its full first public record is
required and digest-bound to the correction action. It is an isolated
fixed-path fixture, not a general or production repeated-history sampler.

## Evidence

The [preregistered contract](../../manifests/j6p-first-record-aware-e2-adapter-2026-09-25.json),
[deterministic result](../../results/j6p-first-record-aware-e2-adapter-2026-09-25.json),
[adapter](../../scripts/j6p_fixed_path_e2_adapter.py),
[12-cell runner](../../scripts/run_j6p_first_record_aware_e2_adapter.py), and
[tests](../../scripts/test_j6p_first_record_aware_e2_adapter.py) pin the
[J6O full public law](j6o-full-binary-sequential-public-record-2026-09-25.md)
and the frozen five-round caller by SHA-256. The matrix covers two first green
outcomes, defer/partial/matched actions, and both independent second binary
keys. For each first/action pair the two keys exhaust the two equal-weight
J6O second records; defer returns no E2. All 12 calls replay exactly and
public completions contain only the whitelisted trial/round, binding digests,
public action edges, and full-binary second record or null. Eight negative
controls reject first-record and digest tampering, action swaps, unsupported
actions, invalid keys, malformed records, and future/private injection.
The old caller is byte-identical; 170 Lab 005 tests pass. No stochastic
histories, schedule-arm evaluations or bootstrap replicates were generated.

The two first-result values happen to induce identical conditional second
probabilities on this path. First-record input is therefore an interface and
causal-binding requirement here, not evidence of an observed probability
shift with first outcome.

## Status

Passed at its fixed-path bound. This does not establish a general D4
action-conditioned instrument, a noisy readout model, or integration with the
phenomenological five-round history caller. Schedule risks, JIT advantage and
threshold claims remain outside this result. The next gate is a separately
registered operator-derived extension to more than this one path.
