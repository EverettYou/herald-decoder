## Summary

On one selected ideal D4 first record, every next charge bit under both
corrections and two held-fixed later faults is determined algebraically
from the full second measurement, except one exactly fair bit. This
reconstructs four complete next-public laws within the existing budget.
The same-future action contrast has exact total variation `15/16` with
identical next flux; it is an action effect on a finite ideal record,
not a decoding or noisy-schedule benefit.

## Evidence

The [prospective four-cell contract](../../manifests/j7p-next-operator-relation-matrix-2026-09-26.json)
freezes J7M's full second laws, J7N's two fixed later-fault keys and
J7O's fair anticommuting witness. It permits complete next-law
reconstruction *only* if each eligible next operator is an exact
signed repeat of a measured second operator or the sole fair witness.
An operator that merely commutes remains unresolved; multiple fair
bits would require their joint correlation. Private physical/future
supports and operators are derivation inputs, not public decoder data.

The [machine-readable result](../../results/j7p-next-operator-relation-matrix-2026-09-26.json)
passes seven input pins before and after, 1,936 cross-round operator-pair
checks and all 68 positive second-prefix/future checks. In every cell,
21 of 24 next sites are exact signed repeats, two are flux-ineligible
and have charge zero, and site 1 is the sole fair anticommuting bit.
There are no unresolved sites. Combining each second-prefix mass with
the two equiprobable next-site-1 outcomes yields normalized complete
next laws. Under later fault `[0]`, baseline has two equiprobable
records and the loop action has 32; under `[4]`, they have four and 64.
For both fixed faults the next public flux matches between actions,
and the exact full-public total variation is `15/16`. The audit
reconstructs 136 prefix-conditional public rows and evaluates no new
Born moment, noisy history, policy arm or bootstrap replicate. Three
focused tests cover the four-cell result, no-promotion-on-commutation
control and deterministic replay.

A separately [preregistered direct Born spot-check](../../manifests/j7q-direct-born-spotcheck-2026-09-27.json)
then tests three fixed probes by the ordered three-block projector
moment expansion, rather than by signed-repeat/anticommutator rules.
Its [exact result](../../results/j7q-direct-born-spotcheck-2026-09-27.json)
replays next charge-0 conditional probabilities `1/2`, `1`, `1/2`
for a variable-witness case, a deterministic repeat and a fixed-witness
case, respectively. It uses 33,024 ordered-moment terms, below the
prior 65,536 ceiling, with eight pins and no noisy work. This checks
representative algebraic branches, not all positive prefixes or the
entire next joint law by direct expansion.

## Status

This is an exact algebraic reconstruction within the frozen ideal
projector model, with three matching direct Born spot-checks but not an
exhaustive direct Born evaluation of the next joint law. The selected
physical error and first record do not constitute
a private-error ensemble or unconditional paired logical-risk
denominator. The long correction is not known to be emitted by the
frozen JIT policy; neither information gain, recovery benefit nor
noisy five-round schedule performance is established.
