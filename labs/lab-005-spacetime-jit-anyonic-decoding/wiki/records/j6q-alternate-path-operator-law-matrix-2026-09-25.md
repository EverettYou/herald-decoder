## Summary

Two preregistered red-error geometries beyond the J6O shared-green pair now
have exact causal first/action/second 24-site public laws on the same ideal
periodic L=2 vacuum orbit. A shared-blue pair transfers the one-random-first,
correlated-two-random-second pattern to the opposite color. A disjoint pair
has a deterministic vacuum charge record in every measured branch. Neither
tested geometry shows a change of second conditional probabilities with the
first result. This is a three-geometry *finite fixture*, not a general D4
transition kernel.

The previously pinned `[0,4]` shared-green case has the analogous first
green:1 and second blue-pair pattern. This is a pattern comparison, not a
proof of global color symmetry. In `[0,1]`, both first-result values give
the same second conditional law; in `[0,3]`, there is only one first result.
Thus these two extensions do not yet demonstrate first-outcome-dependent
probabilities, though the public interface must still causally bind the
actual first record. The full 173-test Lab 005 suite passes. The old caller
is byte-identical; no sampled histories, schedule arms or bootstraps exist.

## Status

Passed at the two-geometry exact bound. These ideal two-edge cases do not
identify a general repeated D4 physical channel, noisy readout law, or JIT
schedule risk. The next discriminating study should preregister a more
complex path/branch geometry that can test genuinely multi-site first
