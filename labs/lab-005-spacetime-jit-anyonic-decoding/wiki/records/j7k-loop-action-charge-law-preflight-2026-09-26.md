## Summary

The proposed loop-toggled red-X correction differs from the baseline by
one homologically trivial six-edge cycle on the exact paper-normalized
periodic L=2 graph. Both actions have the same public correction boundary.
At the fixed selected first record, however, the loop-toggled residual
has five nondeterministic second charge sites, exceeding the registered
three-site cap for a complete second public law. The candidate's joint
charge effect remains uncomputed.

## Evidence

The [preregistered preflight](../../manifests/j7k-loop-action-charge-law-preflight-2026-09-26.json)
uses the [same-flux geometric candidate](j7j-charge-risk-estimand-feasibility-2026-09-26.md),
the fixed three-edge physical error, and the same selected complete first
record of exact mass `1/4`. It pins the periodic embedding, vacuum orbit,
Lab 004 topology classifier, action-token source and unchanged five-round
caller before evaluation.

The [machine result](../../results/j7k-loop-action-charge-law-preflight-2026-09-26.json)
reconciles all 36 red-edge IDs and blue/green endpoint pairs across the
two laboratory representations. The difference cycle is one closed
nonbranching component with no torus winding vector; the topology engine
therefore assigns its two ordinary colour-parity constraints. This
clears the narrow ground-state-relative winding gate, not a full D4
logical-risk or physical-policy gate.

The exact action-conditioned *one-site* second-star marginals identify
only site `0` as nondeterministic after baseline correction `[0]`, but
sites `0,17,20,21,22` after loop-toggled correction
`[1,30,32,34,35]`. Eligibility and same-sector commutation pass in both
residuals. Five variable sites exceed the frozen cap of three, so no
complete second or next-first joint Born law was evaluated. The private
state-token interface can whitelist a declared public action, but the
frozen causal JIT policy has not been shown to emit this long correction.
Seven immutable pins, deterministic replay and two focused tests pass;
the preflight used 1,412 ordered moment terms and no noisy histories,
schedule arms or bootstrap replicates.

## Status

The loop is a topologically permissible *ideal Pauli intervention* at
this narrow level, but the desired charge-specific comparison is still
censored by the registered complete-law cap and is not a schedule-policy
comparison. A separate prospective contract may test a smaller equal-flux
loop candidate or a justified higher cap; this result cannot silently
increase the cap, infer a charge-information gain from one-site marginals,
or rehabilitate withdrawn logical-risk evidence.
