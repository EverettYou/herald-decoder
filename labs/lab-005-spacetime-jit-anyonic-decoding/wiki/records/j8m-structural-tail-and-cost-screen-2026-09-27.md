## Summary

On the fixed ideal L=2 orbit and `p_X=1/10` IID red-X prior, exact first-star one-site constraints sharply tighten all four previously registered omitted-error posterior bounds. The largest is now `0.002522009558` after the all-zero-charge record; the other three are `0.000702020018`, `0.000702020018` and `0.000067384877`. These are *upper bounds*, not evaluated omitted first laws. A direct exact first-mass matrix for all 54 eight-edge supports under the three alternate records would require 86,272 terms, above the inherited 65,536-term ceiling, and was not run.

## Evidence

The [prospective two-branch contract](../../manifests/j8m-structural-tail-and-cost-screen-2026-09-27.json) holds the physical prior, selected first flux, four complete corrected public records, two relation-free actions and compute ceilings fixed. It registers a read-only structural bound over all 8,192 same-flux private errors and a three-record cost projection. After an initial arithmetic result, a direct pairwise commutation assertion was added for every error's 22 same-round first-star operators; the initial [machine file](../../results/j8m-structural-tail-and-cost-screen-2026-09-27.json) is withheld as incomplete integrity provenance, not active evidence. The separately named [verified result](../../results/j8m-structural-tail-and-cost-screen-verified-2026-09-27.json) passes that added assertion, reproduces all prior core numbers exactly, and stays below 60 CPU seconds with zero new Born terms, first masses, second laws, histories, schedule arms or bootstraps.

For a complete first record, its commuting joint projector is dominated by every constituent one-site projector. Thus a deterministic-plus site required to carry charge makes the record impossible; otherwise any fair site bounds its likelihood by `1/2`. Applied to the omitted supports in each branch and combined with the [known exact joint mass](../../results/j8l-first-record-tail-certificate-2026-09-27.json), this gives rigorous worst-case posterior-tail upper bounds. The site-1 and site-2 alternate records each exclude 2,047 of 8,179 omitted errors; the two-charge record excludes 3,070. Exact prior-weighted bounds are stored as rational numbers in the verified result. For the 54 eight-edge supports, the projected direct exact first-mass costs are 28,800, 28,800 and 28,672 terms for the three alternate records. Their 86,272-term sum exceeds the registered cap, so no such full matrix was evaluated.

## Status

For each of these four exact public first records and each fixed action, the full-prior second-public law is within the stated tail bound in total variation of the common known-subset law, regardless of the unknown component's law. This is a bounded-record robustness statement, not a zero-information theorem or an evaluated full mixture. Other first-charge records and first-flux sectors still prevent an overall IID-channel information or risk conclusion; noisy JIT, L=3 and threshold claims remain unavailable. Any new exact likelihood work must be separately registered within the inherited cap or scientifically justified with a new budget decision.
