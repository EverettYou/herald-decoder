## Record

# J4 deterministic phase-transition lifecycle preflight

## Result

The abstract Lyons–Brown schedule can now drive a deterministic, explicitly
labelled phase lifecycle. A valid `ungauge` transition from D(S3) or a boundary
opens one cluster-owned D(Z3) region and records the same supplied fusion
outcome on both sides of the transition. This is an equality invariant over a
fixture value; it is not a simulation of the ungauging circuit.

A current-round e/m parity measurement is then bound to the active region,
its owner, and the preserved deterministic outcome. A matching neutral record
allows `correct_regauge` to terminate the absorber exactly once. A matching
non-neutral record allows `defer_nonneutral` while leaving the region active.
Wrong action order, duplicate regions, repeated closure, and inconsistent
parity records are rejected.

## Verification

Six focused fixtures cover phase opening and outcome preservation, neutral
termination, non-neutral persistence, deterministic record disagreement,
invalid ordering/duplication, and a full public abstract schedule trace from
ungauge through measurement to re-gauge. All 46 current Lab 005 tests pass.

## Claim boundary

This verifies lifecycle and record consistency only. The supplied fusion
outcome is not derived from a D(S3) circuit; parity measurements are not
generated by a noisy physical model; and detector clusters are still supplied
to the schedule. No microscopic reproduction, noisy D4 translation, schedule
comparison, numerical threshold, or fault-tolerance result follows.

The earliest missing causal dependency is now detector-cluster construction
from observation records, including age, extent, separation, and candidate
links to active absorbers. That must pass before a noisy-measurement pilot.
