## Summary

The smallest registered three-edge chain contrast with random second outcomes
does not discriminate measurement-history memory in the complete next-first
public law. All four matched first records give exact total variation zero.
This is a finite ideal-projector negative control, not a theorem of general
state sufficiency or a noisy schedule result.
