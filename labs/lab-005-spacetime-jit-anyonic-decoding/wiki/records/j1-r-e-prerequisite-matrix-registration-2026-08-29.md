# J1 R/E prerequisite matrix registration

Date: 2026-08-29

## Program role

This registration resumes Lab 005 at the physical-observation gate. The G
generic channel has already verified probability, causality, and software
invariants, but it cannot supply the physical semantics required for a
fault-tolerance experiment. The next work therefore tests the earliest missing
premise in each of the two still-distinct physical branches.

## Source search and consequence

The local primary-source archive was searched on 2026-08-29 across:

- Lyons and Brown, *Quantum Computing with Anyons Is Fault Tolerant*, for the
  D(S3) local circuit, detector, absorber, ungauging, neutrality, and linking
  construction;
- Jing *et al.*, *Intrinsic Heralding and Optimal Decoders for Non-Abelian
  Topological Order*, for the D4 fusion record and its explicit
  perfect-measurement scope; and
- Jing *et al.*, *Integer Linear Programming Decoder for Abelian and
  Non-Abelian Topological Codes*, for the structural separation of spatial
  errors, temporal readout errors, and measured-syndrome-dependent fusion
  channels.

The sources do not justify one combined generator. They instead support two
independent prerequisite diagnostics and one dependent future step.

## Registered matrix

### R0 — D(S3) source event catalogue

Compile only source-cited single-fault mappings in three local contexts: bulk
D(S3), the gauging boundary, and ungauged D(Z3). Each row must declare time and
detector support plus phase ownership. Missing circuit detail is a valid
negative result and must remain unresolved rather than being filled from the
D4 or generic channel.

### E0 — perfect-limit D4 temporal composition

Wrap the verified Lab 004 two-call observation-only interface in a maximum
three-round deterministic state transition. The test asks whether the first
action and later physical post-action measurement can be temporally ordered,
digested, and exposed causally without the hidden physical chain. It does not
add noisy readout.

### E1 — dependent noisy D4 instrument

E1 is not yet executable. It requires E0 plus an explicit basis for the
measurement state update. The G confusion matrix may later implement a declared
phenomenological readout layer, but it cannot decide whether the physical
record is persistent, projectively refreshed, or action-dependent.

## Fork classification and budget

R0 and E0 are independent, reversible, in-scope diagnostics. Their combined
registered budget is below four CPU minutes and 1 GiB, uses only existing tiny
fixtures, and produces no stochastic samples. Therefore both will run; no
Discussion thread is needed to rank them. A failure pauses only its dependent
branch.

## Claim boundary

This registration is a research plan, not evidence that either physical model
has been reproduced. It authorizes no pilot comparison, LER, threshold,
schedule advantage, or fault-tolerance conclusion.
