## Summary

Superseded public-record artifact: embedded first records inherited J8A's incorrect `vacuum` bits. The exact second-law rows and comparisons were replayed in the [corrected replacement](j8k-first-public-vacuum-remediation-2026-09-27.md); this page and its original machine file are provenance only.

The complete second public law remains identical between the base error and every candidate error that can produce the *same* one of the four tested first records, under either fixed public action. This extends the selected all-plus result to all base-positive first records on the same ideal orbit. It does not say the first record is uninformative: some candidate errors cannot produce particular first records, and candidate first masses differ.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j8b-alternate-first-complete-second-laws-2026-09-27.json) fixes thirteen private errors, four complete first binary records, two relation-free actions and one L=2 D4 orbit. Its signed first-projector moments are shared across the four first records; all 22 eligible second sites are audited before any complete-law reconstruction. Zero first-mass cells are excluded, not assigned a conditional law. The inherited ceilings are 65,536 orbit moments, 200,000 algebraic-relation probes and 60 CPU seconds.

The [exact machine result](../../results/j8b-alternate-first-complete-second-laws-2026-09-27.json) represents all 104 matrix cells: 96 complete positive-first second laws and eight action cells excluded from four zero-first-mass pairs. All 26 all-plus laws replay J7Z exactly. Every positive law is a normalized rational distribution over full 24-site flux/charge/vacuum records; each action has one or five fair second sites, and deterministic repeated bits are retained. Across all 64 positive *alternative-first candidate/action* cells, total variation from the matched base law is exactly zero. The engine used 1,956 first and 18,284 second orbit moments, plus 20,628 algebraic probes; no ordered projector expansion, history, schedule arm or bootstrap ran. Input and runner pins pass before and after.

## Status

For a prior restricted to these thirteen errors and conditioned on one of the four tested first records, the second record under either fixed relation-free action contributes no additional error-identity information: its likelihood is error-independent wherever the first-record mass is positive. The first record itself can remain informative, and this does not cover other first outcomes possible only under candidate errors, the full 36-edge IID channel, unconditional logical risk, another orbit/geometry, a policy-emitted action, noisy JIT or a threshold. A separately registered broader-support or geometry discriminator is needed before any full-IID or logical-risk conclusion.
