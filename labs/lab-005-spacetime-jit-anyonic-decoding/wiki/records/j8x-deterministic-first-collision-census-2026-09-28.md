## Summary

Direct exact measurement of every equal-prior three-edge collision sector whose canonical error gives a deterministic complete all-zero-charge first record rejects naive structural pooling. All 12 pairs share first-record likelihood one for both errors, but their two-action second-law total-variation patterns split into eight `(0,0)`, two `(3/4,3/4)` and two `(0,1/2)`. Thus the positive pairwise witness is reproducible elsewhere in the class but not universal, and the public action changes informativeness in two sectors.

## Evidence

The [prospective class contract](../../manifests/j8x-deterministic-first-collision-census-2026-09-28.json) selects all 12, rather than a post-result sample, from the 120 exact double-support three-edge first-flux sectors. Each pair's full 24-site first public record, both fixed relation-free charge actions and the remediated ideal D4 instrument are frozen. The [machine-readable direct matrix](../../results/j8x-deterministic-first-collision-census-2026-09-28.json) contains 24 independently replayed first likelihoods and 48 normalized complete action-conditioned second laws, with one independent direct second moment per law. Its earlier selected positive row replays exactly. The class census uses 755 signed-affine terms, 24 direct first terms, 48 second-moment checks and `0.199648` CPU seconds; source/hash, geometry, commutation, action binding, normalization and all budget gates pass. No histories, schedule arms or bootstraps were generated.

## Status

This exact finite class is closed. Matching physical error weight, first flux and complete first-record likelihood do not determine an action-conditioned second law. The result falsifies pooling all 12 sectors by those invariants alone; it does not rule out a finer validated operator equivalence. It does not measure other first records, the remaining 108 collision sectors, all physical errors, full-IID information, logical risk, noisy JIT or a threshold. A next bounded reduction must condition at least on additional action/operator structure and validate against direct laws before reuse.
