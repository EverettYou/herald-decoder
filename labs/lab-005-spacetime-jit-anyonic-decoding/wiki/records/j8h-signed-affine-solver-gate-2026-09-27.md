## Summary

The binary-constraint shortcut now has an exact signed implementation validated against existing complete public laws. The nine supports left unresolved by the earlier cap have both action-conditioned singleton/site-count cells checked and satisfy the inherited five-variable-site gate. Their complete second public laws have **not** been computed.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j8h-signed-affine-solver-gate-2026-09-27.json) freezes the same ideal L=2 orbit, selected complete first public record, four previously exact-law supports (base plus three winding-class representatives), two fixed public actions and the nine uncosted supports. Gaussian elimination finds the affine first-subset masks that satisfy the orbit-character constraint; the ordered-product signs are summed only on those masks. For the four controls it permits exact Walsh reconstruction solely to replay the eight saved complete laws. For the nine other supports it permits only first masses, all 22 eligible second-site singleton means and variable-site counts.

The [machine result](../../results/j8h-signed-affine-solver-gate-2026-09-27.json) reproduces all eight prior full laws exactly, including every nonzero public charge row, deterministic bits, variable sites, first masses and action-bound second fluxes. The nine new supports each have one variable second site under the first action and five under the second; all 18 site gates pass. The full calculation used 1,560 signed affine terms and 512 direct-moment spot checks, well within the inherited 65,536-term and 60-CPU-second bounds. Input and runner hashes passed before and after. No new complete second law, history, schedule arm or bootstrap was evaluated.

## Status

This closes the two J8G prerequisites—the signed solver and the nine site counts—at their registered bounds. The next transition may *separately preregister* the all-54-support complete second-law matrix with unchanged physical, public-interface, action, work and integrity gates. A correct and affordable exact method is not yet evidence about private-error information or logical risk. Other first records, the full physical-error prior, noisy JIT, L=3 and threshold inference remain outside this gate.
