## Summary

An exact algebraic reuse audit still cannot cover all 54 eight-edge supports under the inherited 65,536-work ceiling. It completed first-mass, singleton-second-charge and structural cost checks for 45 supports under both fixed actions, then exhausted the distinct Z-character cap. Fixed nonselective batching without reuse also fails the unchanged *total* work cap on the earlier processed prefix alone.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j8f-orbit-character-reuse-audit-2026-09-27.json) freezes the ideal L=2 orbit, selected full-binary first record, all 54 supports, two action-bound second public fluxes, 65,536 distinct orbit-character evaluations, 200,000 relation lookups, 60 CPU seconds and 512 direct-moment identity replays. The cache composes each ordered operator product to a sign and Z mask, caches the orbit expectation by mask and indexes first-projector relations by orbit-flip parity syndrome. Higher-order second moments are *not* evaluated; their distinct character requirements are projected only.

The [machine result](../../results/j8f-orbit-character-reuse-audit-2026-09-27.json) closes 45 paired supports and 90 action cells, including all 40 earlier singleton rows and the three representative replay controls. First masses, second fluxes and variable-site sets replay; 512 direct moments match the cached signed product. Actual distinct character evaluations reach exactly 65,536 at the next support, while the processed-prefix projected distinct-character union for a future full-law calculation already reaches 179,885. Nominal expansion counters are separate from distinct-character work. The previously measured nonreuse expanded-work prefix is 187,936 terms, already above the total 65,536-term cap; dividing it into fixed batches does not lower that sum. All source/result hash checks passed. CPU use was below one second, so the stop was the work cap, not runtime. Nine supports remain uncosted in this audit. No complete second law, history, schedule arm or bootstrap ran.

## Status

This rejects both tested ways of covering all 54 supports *under the inherited caps*: exact mask caching with this structural expansion, and nonreuse batching at fixed total work. It is not a proof that no more compact identity or analytic symmetry treatment exists, nor a class-wide information null. Further all-support law computation needs a separately bounded and validated compression method or an explicit resource decision; do not silently increase the cap. Full-IID information, logical risk, noisy JIT, L=3 and threshold claims remain outside this result.
