## Summary

The finite ideal action-conditioned public-record contrast has one fixed
private initial physical error. It cannot by itself establish information
about an *unknown* error or an unconditional logical-risk advantage.
The two questions were checked separately on the same frozen sources.

## Evidence

The [prospective two-branch contract](../../manifests/j7r-private-error-estimand-identifiability-audit-2026-09-27.json)
pins the existing exact-law and five-round-pilot artifacts. The
[machine audit](../../results/j7r-private-error-estimand-identifiability-audit-2026-09-27.json)
passed all five hashes and audited both branches without a new Born
moment, error draw, history, schedule arm or bootstrap.

For information, the [complete second law](../../results/j7m-five-site-complete-second-charge-law-2026-09-26.json)
and [complete next law](../../results/j7p-next-operator-relation-matrix-2026-09-26.json)
are conditioned on private initial red-X error `[0,4,3]` and one
first public record of mass `1/4`. The two later fault keys `[0]` and
`[4]` are *future exogenous faults*, not competing initial-error
hypotheses. The represented initial-error support has size one. Hence
information about that fixed error is trivially zero inside this
degenerate fixture, even though the two public action laws differ by
total variation `15/16`. This says nothing about mutual information
under a nondegenerate physical-error prior.

For risk, there are no ideal-fixture rows scored under both actions
over an unconditional attempted-history denominator. The separate
[five-round pilot](../../manifests/j6-d4-matched-history-stochastic-pilot-2026-09-19.json)
defines a useful paired Boolean-union scoring pattern, but its
phenomenological transition channel is not the ideal projector model.
Its numerical histories cannot fill or be pooled with the missing
ideal-fixture risk rows.

## Status

Both estimands are **unidentified from existing evidence**. The next
smallest information prerequisite is a prospectively frozen,
same-first-record error pair with positive weights from one declared
physical channel and complete matched public laws for both actions.
Any full-channel *unconditional* risk comparison additionally needs a
complete or bounded-tail physical ensemble, matched keys, private
Boolean-union scores and an attempted-history denominator. A two-error
toy cannot be relabelled as full-channel risk. The J7P/J7Q finite ideal
action-law result remains valid only at its original boundary.
