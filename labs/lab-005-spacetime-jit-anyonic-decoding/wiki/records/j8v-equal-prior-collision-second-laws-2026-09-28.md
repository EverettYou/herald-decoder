## Summary

An exact three-stratum matched matrix gives a finite positive witness: two equal-prior three-edge errors with the same complete first public record have different complete conditional second-public laws. In the stratum with deterministic first record, total variation is `3/4` under **each** of the two unchanged public actions. In the first-likelihood-`1/2` stratum, total variation is `0` under the singleton action and `1/2` under the five-edge action; in the `1/4` stratum it is `0` under both. The second record is therefore informative for at least one restricted matched pair, and action choice can change that finite contrast. This is not a full-channel information or Bayes-risk result.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j8v-equal-prior-collision-second-laws-2026-09-28.json) freezes the remediated ideal periodic L=2 D4 public interface, `p_X=1/10`, all-zero-charge full-binary first record, public actions `[0]` and `[1,30,32,34,35]`, and deterministic maximum-exact-prior sector selection in each canonical-base first-likelihood stratum `1`, `1/2`, `1/4`. All 7,140 three-edge supports give 120 double-support first-flux sectors: 12, 72 and 36 in those strata. Both private errors in each selected sector have equal IID prior and the same first flux; their private supports never choose an action.

The [exact result](../../results/j8v-equal-prior-collision-second-laws-2026-09-28.json) evaluates six complete first likelihoods and all 12 complete conditional action-bound second public laws. The selected pairs are `[0,5,16]` versus `[2,4,15]` (first likelihood `1` each), `[0,1,30]` versus `[32,34,35]` (`1/2` each), and `[0,1,32]` versus `[30,34,35]` (`1/4` each). Their respective total-variation pairs under the singleton/five-edge public actions are `(3/4,3/4)`, `(0,1/2)`, and `(0,0)`. First-law ordered-projector replay, 12 independent direct second-moment checks, eligible-site/commutation/action-binding gates, nonnegative complete-law normalization and pinned hashes all pass. The run uses 230 signed-affine terms plus 14 direct first terms and 12 direct second checks in 0.116909 CPU seconds; no history, schedule arm or bootstrap was produced.

## Status

The preregistered direct matrix is closed. It rejects a universal finite-pair null for the second public record under these fixed actions and demonstrates action-dependent informativeness in one stratum. It does not prove an operator automorphism, characterize the other 117 collision sectors, integrate omitted errors or the `0.487061947808388` prior in unreached sectors, or estimate full-IID information, unconditional logical risk, noisy JIT or a threshold. A next bounded step should test whether this finite witness survives exact same-flux posterior weighting or validate a law reduction before broad claims.
