## Summary

On the unchanged periodic L=2 geometry, physical red-X error and selected
complete first record, none of the 12 possible simple six-edge correction
loops fits the existing three-variable-second-site cap. This is an exact
finite feasibility result, not a charge-information or logical-risk test.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j7l-alternative-same-flux-loop-screen-2026-09-26.json)
froze exhaustive graph-simple six-edge cycle enumeration, Lab 004 homology,
the J7K physical error `[0,4,3]`, baseline public correction `[0]`, first
record of mass `1/4`, 60 CPU seconds, 65,536 ordered moment terms and the
*unchanged* three-variable-site law cap. Every candidate was evaluated,
including the prior loop as a replay control; no new first record or larger
loop was selected after inspecting results.

The [machine-readable exact screen](../../results/j7l-alternative-same-flux-loop-screen-2026-09-26.json)
reconciles all 36 red-edge IDs and endpoints with Lab 004. It finds 12
unique simple six-edge cycles, all homologically trivial and hence
equal-boundary action toggles. Their conditional one-site second-star
marginals show five, six or seven nondeterministic sites, so zero loops
pass the three-site gate. Baseline repeats its sole variable site `0` and
the previous loop repeats variable sites `0,17,20,21,22`. Exact source
pins, two focused tests and deterministic replay pass. The calculation
used 9,156 ordered moment terms and no complete joint second/next law,
stochastic history, schedule arm, bootstrap or five-round caller edit.

## Status

This closes only the *shortest simple loops* on one fixed initial-error
and first-observation fixture. The result neither rules out longer loops
or other fixtures nor supplies a joint charge effect. A prospective
resource/feasibility analysis must precede any higher-cap exact law;
raising the J7K or J7L registered cap post-data is forbidden. Even a
future complete ideal law would not establish emission by the frozen
causal JIT policy or a matched expected-risk benefit.
