## Summary

For both preselected five-edge candidate errors and both fixed public
actions, the complete second public law is exactly the same as for the
three-edge base error at the selected complete first record. Under each
*separate* base-versus-candidate pair-restricted IID prior, this second
record adds exactly zero information about the binary private error
identity. This finite conditional null does not concern logical risk,
the full IID ensemble, other first records, or noisy JIT schedules.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j7w-pair-information-audit-2026-09-27.json)
fixes two base-versus-peer binary hypotheses, the same complete first
public record, both relation-free actions, `p_X=1/10`, exact first
masses `1/4` and `1/16`, and the resulting candidate posterior
`1/325` before the second record. The [machine audit](../../results/j7w-pair-information-audit-2026-09-27.json)
passes four input pins before and after, checks complete public
flux/charge/vacuum keys and normalized rational laws, and evaluates
all four registered pair/action cells with zero new Born terms,
histories, policy arms or bootstraps.

In every cell the two likelihood dictionaries match record by
record. The full-law total variation is exactly `0`, each observed
record leaves the candidate posterior at `1/325`, and conditional
mutual information is `0` bit by both entropy-difference and KL
calculations. Minimum 0–1 *binary error-identity classification*
risk remains `1/325` with or without the second record. The first
action has two supported complete second records; the second has
32. Both counts are shared by the base and candidate laws, but the
null is based on equality of complete record probabilities, not
on count or marginal similarity alone.

## Status

This is a negative result for the two selected restricted pairs on
one first-record slice. It rules out using the two fixed actions'
second public record to distinguish these particular candidate
initial errors after that first observation. It does not establish
that no other physical error, geometry, first record, action, or
full-IID ensemble can carry information. Classification of exact
error identity is not ground-state-relative logical-failure scoring,
and the five-round caller remains frozen. The next in-scope
prerequisite is a prospectively bounded search for a genuinely
different same-first-record error-conditioned second law, without
relabeling the large action effect as information gain.
