## Summary

The only second/next anticommuting witness in each of four matched
action/future cells yields an exact fair next charge at one site after
every positive complete second record. This algebraic result avoids the
current brute-force exact engine but does not determine the complete next
record or a decoding benefit.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j7o-witness-projector-reduction-2026-09-26.json)
freezes the two prior public actions, both private later-fault keys, the
selected first record, all 34 prior positive complete second records,
six input hashes and a zero-Born budget. The
[machine-readable audit](../../results/j7o-witness-projector-reduction-2026-09-26.json)
checks 2,860 same-round/cross-round operator pairs and 68
action/future-prefix combinations. Six pins pass before and after;
same-round second operators commute and all checked witness operators
are Hermitian involutions in the frozen bitmask representation.

Under later fault `[0]`, measured second site 0 anticommutes with next
site 1. Under `[4]`, measured second site 2 anticommutes with next site
1; site 2 has fixed positive charge in every complete second record,
but remains measured. In either case, for its recorded second outcome
`a`, `P_a=(I+aA)/2` and `{A,B}=0` imply `P_a B P_a=0`. Hence each
positive post-second state has next witness mean zero and conditional
next-site-1 charge probabilities exactly `1/2,1/2`. Both action cells
share this identity for each held-fixed future key. The audit evaluates
zero Born moments or next joint laws, noisy histories, policy arms and
bootstrap replicates. Two focused tests replay the result.

## Status

This is an exact *one-site conditional* law within the ideal projector
model. It cannot determine another next-site marginal, a correlation
among next sites or with the second record, the complete next-public
law, information about hidden error, logical risk, policy emission or
noisy JIT benefit. A further bounded reduction or targeted exact test
is required before any full-law comparison; the previous 65,536-term
ceiling has not been raised.
