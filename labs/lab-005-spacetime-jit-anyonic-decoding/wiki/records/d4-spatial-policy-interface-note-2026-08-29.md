# D4 spatial-policy interface note

## Result

The Lab 004-to-005 boundary is now registered as an observation-only, two-call policy interface. Lab 005 owns the causal history and commit/defer decision; Lab 004 receives a committed spatial snapshot, returns the flux correction, and may receive a later post-action measurement record only after that correction has been physically applied. Simulation truth and logical scoring remain in a separate sidecar.

The contract is [`manifests/j1-d4-spatial-policy-interface-manifest-2026-08-29.json`](../../manifests/j1-d4-spatial-policy-interface-manifest-2026-08-29.json). It freezes the validated Lab 004 source hashes, names every decoder-visible input and action output, forbids future/truth fields, and registers causal, syndrome-fidelity, support, replay, and anti-leakage gates.

## Important boundary found during registration

The existing Lab 004 end-to-end simulator is not itself a deployable online API. Its `decode_physical_error` routine accepts the hidden physical chain so that it can score the flux correction and derive the simulated post-flux relation graph. Lab 005 must therefore not call that routine. A dedicated adapter must expose only the measured flux/charge record at stage 1, then accept a post-action measurement layout generated by the physical protocol rather than reconstructing that layout from hidden truth.

R6AH later withdrew the R5a finite-size baseline claim: its first and second
records expose support sentinels, and its charge boundary mixes public action
with private relations. The two-call serializer/adapter gate below remains a
structural interface test, but it must not cite R5a numerical performance as
validation. A corrected Lab 004 cohort must use the remediated R6AF public
record and relation-free charge action before supplying a spatial-performance
baseline to Lab 005.

## Implemented gate

The serializers and two-call adapter now live in
`scripts/d4_spatial_policy.py`. Eight focused tests reject forbidden and
future fields, prove that causal-prefix digests ignore later rounds, verify
first- and second-stage syndrome fidelity (including a nonempty charge
action), bind the second observation to the first action, reject unsupported
parity, reproduce deterministically, and confirm that neither public entry
point accepts a truth argument. Together with the existing Lab 005 tests,
24/24 tests pass.

This is interface verification under ideal supplied spatial observations. It
does not provide a noisy D4 measurement channel, a JIT schedule result, a
threshold, or fault tolerance.
