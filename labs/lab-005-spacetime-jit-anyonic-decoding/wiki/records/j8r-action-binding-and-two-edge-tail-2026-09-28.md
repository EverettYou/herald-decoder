## Summary

Equal one-edge sector priors and base first-record likelihoods cannot, by themselves, pool the action-conditioned public laws under the two *fixed* correction actions. A relabeling preserving the singleton action must fix edge 0; membership in both actions separates the 36 one-edge bases into at least three classes of sizes 1, 5 and 30. Independently, the highest-prior two-edge sector's specified complete first record has an omitted-error posterior upper bound `0.013452016793`, down from the crude `0.026546923919` prior-only upper bound.

## Evidence

The [prospective two-branch contract](../../manifests/j8r-action-binding-and-two-edge-tail-2026-09-28.json) freezes the ideal periodic L=2 D4 model, `p_X=1/10`, pre-action full-binary first record, fixed public red-edge actions `[0]` and `[1,30,32,34,35]`, action-conditioned second flux, private-error boundary and 60-CPU-second/8,192-error limits. The [verified result](../../results/j8r-action-binding-and-two-edge-tail-2026-09-28.json) checks both fixed actions against all 36 one-edge bases. The singleton action forces edge 0 to remain fixed under any strict action-preserving relabeling; five edges lie only in the second action and 30 in neither. Each action produces 36 distinct second-flux masks across those bases. This is a necessary action-binding obstruction, not a census of geometric automorphisms or second-charge public laws.

For the independent tail branch, the maximum exact two-edge J8P sector prior with a lexicographic tie break prospectively selects private base `[0,1]`. Its [J8Q base first likelihood](../../results/j8q-low-weight-base-first-laws-2026-09-28.json) is exactly `1/2`. All 8,191 other same-flux errors have a fair one-site first-star marginal; all 8,192 compatible errors pass eligible-site and pairwise commutation checks. One-site projector domination gives the omitted posterior upper bound `0.013452016793`, versus `0.026546923919` from the prior-only bound. No omitted complete-record likelihood was evaluated. CPU use was 2.777636 seconds; new Born terms, second laws, histories, schedule arms and bootstraps were zero.

## Status

Both bounded branches passed. Fixed-action pooling of all one-edge sectors is ruled out without an action-covariant public relabeling, and this one high-prior two-edge record has a tighter but nonzero omitted tail. Neither finding proves or refutes a full geometry/operator-law symmetry, supplies other first records or second charge laws, or establishes full-IID information/risk, noisy JIT or a threshold. A further law reduction must carry the action along with any edge/site relabeling and pass public-interface replay.
