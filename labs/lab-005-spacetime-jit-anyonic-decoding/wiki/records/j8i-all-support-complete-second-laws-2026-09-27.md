## Summary

For the selected all-plus complete first public record on the fixed ideal L=2 orbit, all 54 eight-edge same-first-flux candidate errors have exactly the same *complete* action-conditioned second full-binary public law as the base error under each of two fixed public actions. This is an exact result for the registered finite support set and selected first-record slice, not a full physical-error-prior information or logical-risk result.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract and recorded integrity amendment](../../manifests/j8i-all-support-complete-second-laws-2026-09-27.json) freeze all 54 supports (18 in each winding class), the base error, selected 24-site first flux/charge/vacuum arrays, both relation-free red-charge actions, action-bound 24-site second public records, exact signed affine projector sums, and exact total variation against the base under the same action. The first attempt's [withheld machine file](../../results/j8i-all-support-complete-second-laws-2026-09-27.json) generated law rows but executed zero planned direct-moment checks because its chosen site was inadmissible. It is retained as failed integrity provenance only; it does not support this conclusion. The amendment requires an admissible direct check in every action cell and saves a separate replacement.

The [verified replacement result](../../results/j8i-all-support-complete-second-laws-verified-2026-09-27.json) closes the base plus 54 candidates, 110 action-law cells in total. All 108 candidate/action exact total-variation values against the matched base equal zero. Every candidate law has two positive public rows under the first action and 32 under the second. First masses are exact and positive, every full-binary row satisfies the public flux/charge/vacuum convention, and each conditional law is nonnegative and normalized. Eight earlier base/representative laws replay exactly. The replacement executes 110/110 direct-moment checks and 4,719 signed affine terms, below the inherited 65,536-term and 60-CPU-second ceilings; all source/result hashes pass before and after. The two attempts' law-row payload hashes match, but only the replacement passes the full acceptance gate. No stochastic histories, schedule arms or bootstraps ran.

## Status

Conditional on this selected first record and either fixed action, the second complete public record cannot distinguish the base from any of the 54 tested eight-edge errors: every conditional law is identical. Hence it adds zero error-identity information for any prior *restricted to this finite set after that first record*. The first record itself may still be informative because its probability differs across errors (`1/4`, `1/16`, `1/64`, or `1/256`). Other physical-error supports, other first records, the full IID prior, unconditional or logical risk, noisy repeated measurement, L=3 and threshold behavior are not resolved. The next separate study should test whether this selected-slice equality survives integration over a frozen physical-error prior and all relevant first records before making an information or policy claim.
