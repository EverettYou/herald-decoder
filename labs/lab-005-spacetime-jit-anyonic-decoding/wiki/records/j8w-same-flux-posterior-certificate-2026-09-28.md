## Summary

The finite positive equal-prior error-pair contrast is not erased by admitting every other private error with the same complete first flux. For the specified full first public record, the selected pair has at least `99.902965%` posterior mass in its sector; under either frozen public action, the exact conditional second law of one selected error differs from that of its full private-error complement by total variation at least `0.746607`. These are conservative bounds, not evaluated mixture laws or a logical-risk result.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j8w-same-flux-posterior-certificate-2026-09-28.json) reuses the [matched complete-law matrix](../../results/j8v-equal-prior-collision-second-laws-2026-09-28.json) without selecting another error, first record or action. Its [exact arithmetic result](../../results/j8w-same-flux-posterior-certificate-2026-09-28.json) uses the complete physical prior of each selected first-flux sector and bounds every omitted error's probability of the specified complete first record by one. The selected pair posterior lower bounds in the first-likelihood `1`, `1/2` and `1/4` strata are `0.9990296503`, `0.9974504520` and `0.9477533523`. The corresponding worst-case second-law total-variation lower bounds for one selected private error versus its full complement are `(0.746607,0.746607)`, `(0,0.492371)` and `(0,0)` under the singleton/five-edge actions. The bound follows from the reverse triangle inequality; no omitted second law is assumed. Source hashes, equal pair first likelihoods, sector mass and zero new Born/history/arm/bootstrap counters pass in `0.002014` CPU seconds.

## Status

The registered arithmetic certificate is closed. It strengthens the restricted positive information witness even after all same-flux alternatives are admitted. It does not measure the full conditional mixture, a complete-channel mutual information, a ground-state-relative logical Bayes risk, a noisy schedule, scaling or a threshold. The next discriminating question is whether a similarly validated reduction can evaluate other first records and flux sectors within the inherited exact-term budget.
