## Summary

A read-only matrix compared every previously screened alternative
initial error under the same sparse prior and the frozen exact-law
implementation. Two five-edge errors tie for the largest *binary-pair*
information ceiling and are the only candidates with any two-action
complete-second-law term projection below the inherited budget. Their
actual second-variable-site counts and second public laws remain unknown.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j7t-candidate-prior-cost-matrix-2026-09-27.json)
fixes twelve candidate rows, the `p_X=1/10` prior restricted to each
base/candidate pair, both public actions, 22 eligible second sites per
action, and all 36 hypothetical `k_0,k_1=0..5` variable-site count
cells per candidate. The [machine matrix](../../results/j7t-candidate-prior-cost-matrix-2026-09-27.json)
passes five input/source pins before and after, checks all 432 cost
cells, and uses zero new ordered Born moments, histories, arms or
bootstraps. The frozen exact implementation's cost projection includes
one first-record mass, two denominators, 44 one-site checks, and all
binary complete-second assignments; it is not a lower bound on every
possible algorithm.

The registered maximal-entropy, under-budget rule retains two peers:
private initial errors `[2,3,5,15,16]` and `[0,10,11,33,35]`.
Each has selected first-record mass `1/16`, candidate weight `1/325`
after the first record in its *own two-support-restricted* IID prior,
and binary entropy ceiling about `0.030107` bit. For each, the present
two-action exact engine projects at least 23,088 ordered terms, and
stays strictly below 65,536 terms only if both actual second-variable
counts are at most three. The earlier lexicographic seven-edge control
has a `1/104977` pair weight and even its minimum projected cost is
368,832 terms, so it is not promoted to a full-law run at the existing
ceiling.

## Status

This matrix selects two *peer prerequisites*, not a positive
information finding or a decoder action. Next measure the actual
action-conditioned second-variable counts for both five-edge peers
under the same first record, with a prospective bounded gate; only
then decide whether complete second laws fit the inherited budget.
Information about the full IID error ensemble, unconditional logical
risk, policy-emitted JIT correction and noisy schedules remain outside
this result.
