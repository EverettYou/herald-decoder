## Summary

The four previously untested red-X correction subsets of the fixed
three-edge chain yield no first-record dependence on *newly eligible* second
charge sites. Together with the prior prefix controls, this exhausts the
seven nonempty in-support correction subsets of this one ideal physical
chain. Two single-edge actions make the **complete** second public record
depend on the first record, but only because an unchanged star is measured
again. This negative result is not a theorem for other error geometries,
initial states, noisy measurements or repeated rounds.

[exact rational result](../../results/j6t-alternate-action-new-site-law-2026-09-25.json),
[runner](../../scripts/run_j6t_alternate_action_new_site_law.py), and

physical red-X chain `[0,4,3]` and its same four equiprobable first public
records. Each conditional second law is obtained from the ordered projector
sandwich in the actual postselected first sector, then projected onto exactly
defined private analysis site sets. Full published records retain only 24-bit
flux/charge/vacuum arrays; the private site classifications are diagnostics,
not decoder inputs.

| Correction edges | Newly eligible second sites | Full second-record dependence on first | New-only joint dependence on first |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `[3]` | site 3 | Yes: unchanged site 1 repeats | No: site 3 is half/half |

| `[4,3]` | site 3; old site 2 operator changes | No | No: site 3 is half/half |

The joint projection onto newly eligible **plus changed-operator** sites is
also identical across all four first records for each action. All eligible
stars equal flux-free sites and commute in their actual sector; first and
second exact rational distributions are nonnegative and normalize, including
all four positive first branches. The one empty new-site projection under
`[4]` is recorded as vacuous, not positive evidence of independence. The
previously tested `[0]`, `[0,4]`, and `[0,4,3]` actions are documented in the
[repeat/four-site control](j6s-repeat-transfer-four-site-2026-09-25.md);
their new-only laws are likewise first-independent. The original J6R censor
remains intact under its own three-site bound. The exact four-action run used
`0.041` CPU seconds within a 60-second budget, and all 187 Lab tests pass.
No stochastic histories, schedule arms or bootstrap replicates were generated.

## Status

This is a bounded negative result about the fixed ideal `L=2` chain and its
seven in-support correction subsets. It weakens the proposed interpretation
of observed first-dependence as new-site charge transfer in *this* fixture.
It does not show that such transfer is impossible on a branched, looped,
longer, different-sector, or noisy D4 history. The physical five-round JIT
caller remains frozen. A next path-family test needs a separately registered
geometry/model question rather than adaptive extension of this matrix.
