## Summary

The exact second-public-law equality established on 67 known private errors cannot yet be promoted to an IID-prior information null. On the fixed ideal L=2 geometry, 8,192 errors have the selected complete first flux, of which 8,125 lack a complete second law. Their combined prior weight is small at the frozen `p_X=1/10` diagnostic rate, but first-charge selection can reweight it. Other first-flux sectors are outside this audit.

The [verified machine result](../../results/j8j-full-prior-coverage-feasibility-verified-2026-09-27.json) verifies boundary rank 23 and kernel dimension 13, so the selected first-flux sector contains exactly `2^13 = 8,192` distinct physical errors. The 67 known complete-law errors—J7T's twelve shortest-loop alternatives and J8I's base plus 54 eight-edge supports—cover about `0.998804` of the sector's IID prior *weight* at `p_X=1/10`, but only 67/8,192 error identities. The selected first-flux sector itself has prior probability about `3.32617e-5`; all other first-flux sectors remain unaudited here. Exact structural single-site analysis finds between 2 and 22 variable first-charge sites per error. The sum of `2^v` possible per-error first-charge strings is 2,872,423,652, far above the inherited 65,536-term first-projector ceiling. This is an upper structural work count, not a count of positive full joint laws: charge correlations may eliminate strings. The full 36-edge IID weights normalize exactly; all input and runner hashes pass before and after. No first-record probabilities, second laws, histories, arms or bootstraps were evaluated.
