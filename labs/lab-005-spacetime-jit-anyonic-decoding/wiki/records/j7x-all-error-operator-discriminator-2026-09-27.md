## Summary

All twelve pre-existing private physical-error alternatives were crossed
with both fixed public charge actions on the same selected complete first
record. No candidate/action cell has a *resolved* second one-site charge
marginal different from the base. This is a bounded one-site structural
negative screen, not equality of the complete second public laws.

## Evidence

The [preregistered matrix](../../manifests/j7x-all-error-operator-discriminator-2026-09-27.json)
fixes twelve J7T errors, two relation-free actions, a two-action base
control, all 22 eligible second sites per cell, source pins and a
200,000 algebraic-probe/60 CPU-second ceiling. It allows `plus`, `minus`,
`fair`, or `unresolved` classifications. First-projector
anticommutation proves a fair second bit; a commuting orbit-stabilizer
relation proves a deterministic bit. A changed *resolved* bit would prove
a changed complete law, but an unresolved or matched bit cannot prove
law equality.

The [machine result](../../results/j7x-all-error-operator-discriminator-2026-09-27.json)
passes all pins, first/second flux and commuting-operator gates for 12
candidates × 2 actions plus two base cells. It checks its resolved base
labels against the exact base law and its two prior peers against their
exact J7W null. Of 24 candidate/action cells, zero has a resolved
one-site difference from the base. The search used 7,368 algebraic
relation probes, 0 ordered Born projector terms, 0 new histories, 0
policy arms and 0 bootstraps. Under action `[0]`, each cell has 21
resolved deterministic-plus sites; the remaining site is fair or
unresolved. Under action `[1,30,32,34,35]`, each cell has 17
resolved deterministic-plus sites; the other five are fair or
unresolved. The unresolved labels prevent a complete one-site null
claim, even before considering joint correlations.

## Status

This screen found no cheap single-site witness for a different
error-conditioned complete second law on this selected ideal periodic
fixture. It does not rule out a difference in an unresolved marginal,
joint charge correlation, another first record/geometry/action, or a
full-IID information effect. The next independent bounded prerequisite
is a prospective joint-correlation discriminator or a separately
registered different-geometry design; no favorable candidate is
selected after this null. No logical-risk or noisy JIT result follows.

The later [exact singleton/pair test](j7y-low-order-second-moments-2026-09-27.md)
resolves the then-unresolved one-site means as fair and finds no
pair-moment difference; higher-order joint correlations remain open.
