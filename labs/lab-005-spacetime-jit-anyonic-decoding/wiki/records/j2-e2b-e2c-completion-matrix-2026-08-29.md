# J2 E2B/E2C causal completion matrix

The deterministic paper-\(L=2\) completion matrix passes in all eight cells:
syndrome-only and heralded modes under immediate, one-round fixed delay,
bounded JIT, and explicitly noncausal offline scheduling. Every cell preserves
its exact E1 flux action, consumes an action-bound post-flux report, returns a
nonempty blue charge correction, and either advances to the available next
causal prefix or ends at the final recorded round.

The pre-execution diagnostic corrected one impossible phrase in the
registration. The two modes choose different flux actions in every branch, so
a measurement after those actions cannot have one action-independent identity.
The verified matched design instead shares the same pre-action history,
measurement layout, and report rule, while each post-action report is
conditioned on and bound to its own intervention. This changes neither scope
nor budget.

The E2C controls pass. Premature post-action supply, wrong request/action/layout
binding, flux or simultaneous post-action labels, and parity-inconsistent
charge support fail closed. A future-only public change leaves every earlier
causal completed transition unchanged while changing the explicitly noncausal
offline result. A private-truth-only change leaves all eight public cells
unchanged, and replay is bit identical.

Six new E2B/E2C tests pass, all 20 E1/E2 integration tests pass, and the full
Lab 005 suite passes 131/131. No stochastic sample or logical score was
generated.

This is deterministic interface evidence only. It does not establish schedule
advantage, logical-error rate, threshold, hardware fidelity, scalable recovery,
or fault tolerance.
