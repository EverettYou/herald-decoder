## Summary

A closed red-edge loop supplies a geometric way to vary the public
correction while holding the initial error, later fault and next public
flux fixed. This is only a candidate for a charge-specific action-law
test; its logical effect and complete measurement law remain unverified.
Separately, the previous one-error, one-first-record exact calculation
cannot estimate expected logical risk without a physical-error ensemble
and unconditional paired scoring.

## Evidence

The [registered two-branch audit](../../manifests/j7j-charge-risk-estimand-feasibility-2026-09-26.json)
uses the previously verified shortest six-red-edge simple cycle and the
same initial error and two later-fault keys as the
[finite total-action law](j7i-same-future-total-action-law-2026-09-26.md).
It toggles that cycle on baseline public correction edge `0`, yielding
the predeclared alternative correction edges `1,30,32,34,35`.

The [machine result](../../results/j7j-charge-risk-estimand-feasibility-2026-09-26.json)
checks four action/future cells. The cycle's public flux boundary is zero.
Within both fixed-future columns the two actions produce distinct final
red-X supports but identical full 24-site *next public flux* arrays.
This removes the deterministic flux separation that explained the prior
full-public TV of one. It does not yet identify a charge effect: cycle
homology, public-action admissibility, complete first/second/next Born
laws and resource caps require separate validation.

The independent risk branch inspects the existing exact-law design: one
fixed private initial error, one selected first record and conditional
public laws, with no registered physical-error ensemble, unconditional
paired loss rows or denominator, or applied ground-state-relative
Boolean-union score. Thus an expected logical failure difference is not
identified by those data. Five source/result/caller pins, two focused tests
and deterministic replay pass; no Born-law evaluation, stochastic history,
schedule arm or bootstrap was added.

## Status

The smallest next prerequisite for a charge-specific exact law is to
classify the loop's logical/homology action and validate that both
corrections are admissible public interventions under the same selected
first record, then preflight complete-law size. A matched-risk study is
more dependent: it needs a separately frozen physical-error distribution,
paired keys and loss/denominator before sampling. Neither branch merits a
human ranking request, and neither currently supports herald or JIT benefit.
