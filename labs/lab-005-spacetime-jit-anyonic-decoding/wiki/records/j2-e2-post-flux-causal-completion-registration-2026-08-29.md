# J2 E2 post-flux causal completion registration

The research workflow resumes at the earliest unsupported dependency after the
released E1 stage-1 interface: causal post-flux completion. Before any noisy
history or schedule-performance study, the project must connect the exact
stage-1 flux action to a physically supplied, action-bound charge record, the
public second-stage charge action, and a later causal prefix.

This is a prerequisite rather than a choice among competing performance
experiments. Stochastic history generation, logical scoring, and schedule
comparison all consume this boundary, so they remain blocked. The exact
\(D(S_3)\) circuit-reproduction branch remains independently paused at its
previously recorded source gap and does not block the researcher-selected
\(D_4\)-first branch.

The registered matrix starts with E2A, an action-bound typed post-flux adapter.
Only after E2A passes will E2B/E2C run all two public modes by four schedule
branches plus temporal, parity, causality, truth-exclusion, and replay controls.
The budget is deterministic paper-\(L=2\) fixtures, at most four readout rows,
under five CPU minutes and 1 GiB, with no sampling.

Allowed evidence is limited to deterministic two-stage causal information flow
and interface correctness. Logical-error rate, schedule advantage, threshold,
fault tolerance, hardware extraction fidelity, and scalable recovery remain
prohibited.
