## Summary

An exact parity-moment/Walsh reconstruction closed the formerly
cost-censored complete second public laws for both five-edge
alternative initial errors under both frozen actions. It stays below
the inherited computation ceiling and replays the older enumerated
base-error laws. This establishes conditional finite ideal laws, not

base-control replay cells. Its first invocation failed before any

and original runner hash without changing the matrix or caps.

The [machine law](../../results/j7v-joint-second-walsh-reduction-2026-09-27.json)
passes twelve input/source pins before and after, exactly replays both
complete previously enumerated base-control law dictionaries, and
checks one direct all-positive full-assignment Born row in each of
the six cells. Operator-product involution, original-versus-composite
moments, exact rational nonnegativity/normalization, identical first
public record, both complete second flux records, and all frozen
one-site marginals pass. The calculation spent 51,816 ordered moments
under the 55,000 tick cap and the inherited 65,536 ceiling; no
histories, policy arms or bootstraps ran.

For each peer, the selected first-record mass is `1/16`. The first
public action has two positive complete second rows (`1/2` each);
the second has 32 positive complete second rows (`1/32` each).
These are complete 24-site public flux/charge/vacuum records,
including deterministic sites and correlations, not merely the
one-site summaries from the previous preflight.

It does not yet report whether the two error-conditioned laws differ,
nor calculate mutual information or a posterior under the restricted
binary pair. That comparison needs a separately registered analysis
with the frozen pair prior and common first record. Unconditional
Boolean-union logical risk requires a different full-channel
denominator and remains open independently. The frozen five-round
caller is unchanged.
