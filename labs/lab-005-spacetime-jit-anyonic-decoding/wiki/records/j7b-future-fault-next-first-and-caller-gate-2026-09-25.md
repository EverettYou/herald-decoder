## Summary

An exact, nonzero future red-X fault can be propagated through the earlier
first measurement, public correction, and optional second measurement on one
fixed two-edge periodic D4 path. All ten positive prior public prefixes yield
a normalized complete next-first 24-site binary record. A matched-final-support

measurement in this fixture. The frozen five-round caller still cannot consume
