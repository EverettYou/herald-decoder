All first masses, 96 normalized positive second laws, eight zero-mass action exclusions, 26 earlier all-plus law replays, both public actions and candidate/base total variations remain unchanged. The J8C geometry runner consumes only J8B's `complete_second_law_cells` and `different_alternate_candidate_action_cells`, both unchanged; its geometric rows need no numerical regeneration for this representation-only defect. Corrected J8B and J8I together verify the 67 known same-flux complete-law supports used by J8J. All pinned inputs and the remediation runner pass before and after; no stochastic histories, arms or bootstraps were run.
