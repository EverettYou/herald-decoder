## Summary

The next exact physical-law test extends the fixed three-edge red-X chain
by one edge in two ways: a nonbranching five-vertex path and a four-edge
tree with a three-arm junction. Across four preregistered correction actions
per topology, neither the newly eligible second-site joint charge law nor
the new-plus-changed-operator joint law depends on the positive first public
record. Three *complete* second public laws do depend on it, but each has
an unchanged first/second operator that repeats. This finite negative
mechanism result does not establish a general D4 temporal independence law.

## Evidence

The [preregistered contract](../../manifests/j6u-four-edge-path-branch-2026-09-25.json),
[exact result](../../results/j6u-four-edge-path-branch-2026-09-25.json),
[runner](../../scripts/run_j6u_four_edge_path_branch.py), and
[tests](../../tests/test_j6u_four_edge_path_branch.py) pin eight source/prior/
caller hashes and compare path `[0,4,3,7]` with branched tree `[0,4,3,5]`.
The path visits blue:0–green:1–blue:2–green:3–blue:4; the tree instead has
degree three at blue:2. Both have no cycle. The same J6M ideal `L=2` orbit
and exact ordered projector law are used for every branch. The four actions
are correction of edge 0, edge 3, both, and all four physical edges.

| Physical topology | First record | Complete second-law dependence | New/changed-site joint dependence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nonbranching path | Three random first charge sites; eight positive first records of mass `1/8` | Edge 0 and edge 3 actions depend on first through repeated unchanged stars; the two-edge and matched actions do not | None in the three non-vacuous new-site actions; edge 3 has no newly eligible site |
| Branched tree | One random first site; two positive first records of mass `1/2` | Edge 3 action depends on first through an unchanged repeated star; the other three actions do not | None in all four new-site actions |

The matched path has five individually nondeterministic second sites but only
eight positive joint charge strings per first record, each mass `1/8`, with
the same full second distribution for all eight first records. This is why
the complete joint law, not only marginals, is the tested object. All eight
topology/action branches are uncensored, with nonnegative exact rational
first and conditional second masses summing to one. Every eligible star
equals a residual flux-free site and the eligible operators commute in the
actual sector. Public first/second records contain only full 24-bit
flux/charge/vacuum arrays; private physical support and site partitions
stay in analysis metadata. Exact replay and all 193 Lab tests pass. The
matrix used `1.698` CPU seconds under its 60-second cap; it produced zero
stochastic histories, schedule arms or bootstrap replicates.

## Status

Current only for these two ideal four-edge topologies and four named
correction actions on one fixed orbit. The three-edge all-action negative
result and this four-edge comparison weaken the simple hypothesis that any
longer or branched tree automatically transfers first-observation
information to a genuinely new second site. They do not exclude looped
errors, other actions, other logical sectors, noisy readout or hidden-state
memory across multiple measurement rounds. No physical five-round JIT
performance interpretation is released.
