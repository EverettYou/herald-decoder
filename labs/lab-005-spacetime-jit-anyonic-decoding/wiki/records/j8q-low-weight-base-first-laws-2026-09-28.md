## Summary

An exact census of the canonical minimum-weight private error in each of the 667 first-flux sectors reached by at most two red-X edges finds that its specified complete all-zero-charge first-record likelihood is one in 595 sectors and one-half in 72 sectors. The 72 half-likelihood cases are precisely the two-edge, two-flux-bit group. Every exact-prior-equivalent group has one canonical-base likelihood, a necessary but insufficient witness for any full public-law symmetry.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j8q-low-weight-base-first-laws-2026-09-28.json) freezes the earlier [667-sector prior screen](../../results/j8p-low-weight-flux-coset-screen-2026-09-28.json), its unique minimum-weight private representatives, the signal-only pre-action full-binary first record (sector flux, zero charge, complementary vacuum), 60 CPU seconds and at most 65,536 exact projector terms. It requires a complete cost preflight before the first Born call and forbids extra sectors, other first records and any second law. The [verified machine result](../../results/j8q-low-weight-base-first-laws-2026-09-28.json) passes all 667 eligible-site and pairwise first-star commutation checks, exact-rational likelihood bounds, J8O vacuum/edge-0 replay, sector-prior partition and pinned-hash gates. The preflight projected 739 terms; exactly 739 terms were used in 0.224622 CPU seconds. There were 667 positive base first masses, zero second laws, histories, schedule arms or bootstraps.

The sum of disjoint prior-weighted *base contributions* to these 667 specified first records is `0.277850261054250` of the full physical channel. It is a lower bound on the probability of the union of these distinct-flux public records because other private errors may produce the same records; the sum of complete sector priors `0.288680551769476` is an upper bound. The remaining `0.711319448230524` lies in first-flux sectors this census did not reach. Individual one-base omitted-error posterior bounds in the machine result use the crude residual sector prior, not evaluated omitted complete-record likelihoods. Equality of these one-record base likelihoods within 12 exact-prior groups is not equality of all first records, error-conditioned laws, or action-conditioned second records.

## Status

Both preregistered branches closed within budget. The census exposes a simple difference between neighboring two-edge errors and the other minimum-weight bases, but it cannot establish geometry/operator-law symmetry, an all-error first-law mixture, full-IID information/risk, noisy JIT or a threshold. The next bounded prerequisite should test a valid geometry/operator action on matched first and second public records, or tighten prior-weighted omitted laws for a prospectively chosen high-mass group.
