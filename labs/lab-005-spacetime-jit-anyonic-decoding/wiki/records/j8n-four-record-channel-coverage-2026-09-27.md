## Summary

At the frozen ideal L=2 `p_X=1/10` IID physical prior, the four corrected complete first records account for between `93.4853%` and `93.5794%` of their one selected first-flux sector, but only about `0.00311%` of the entire physical channel. At least `6.4206%` of that selected sector remains in other first-charge records, while exactly `99.9966738%` of the channel lies in other first-flux sectors. These are mass-coverage facts, not an information or decoder-risk result.

## Evidence

The [prospective two-branch contract](../../manifests/j8n-four-record-channel-coverage-2026-09-27.json) freezes the normalized 36-edge IID prior, four disjoint corrected full-binary first records, [known exact joint masses](../../results/j8l-first-record-tail-certificate-2026-09-27.json) and [one-site omitted joint-mass upper bounds](../../results/j8m-structural-tail-and-cost-screen-verified-2026-09-27.json). The [verified exact-rational result](../../results/j8n-four-record-channel-coverage-2026-09-27.json) sums the four event lower/upper masses, subtracts from the [selected first-flux prior](../../results/j8j-full-prior-coverage-feasibility-verified-2026-09-27.json), and checks a separate binomial normalization of all 36-edge IID physical errors. Both prior partitions, record distinctness, public `vacuum=1-charge`, pinned hashes and CPU ceiling pass. No new Born term, first or second public law, history, schedule arm or bootstrap was evaluated.

The four-record full-channel probability is bounded between `0.000031094830537` and `0.000031126136499`. Relative to the selected flux, this is between `0.934852563938191` and `0.935793764063273`. Other first-charge records in that same flux therefore retain between `0.064206235936728` and `0.065147436061809` of its prior mass. The exact complementary other-flux probability is `0.999966738251852`. The machine result retains rational endpoints; displayed decimals are rounded.

## Status

The earlier four-record conditional second-law bounds describe a narrow, explicitly selected part of the physical IID ensemble. The coverage calculation rules out promoting those four cases to an overall information-null or logical-risk conclusion. It does not identify the missing first records' second laws, the many other flux sectors' laws, a noisy JIT benefit or any threshold. The next bounded scientific prerequisite should test a representative or symmetry-reduced *other-flux* public-law branch before deciding whether a full-channel enumeration is feasible under the inherited cap; an arbitrary first-record selection cannot stand in for the physical mixture.
