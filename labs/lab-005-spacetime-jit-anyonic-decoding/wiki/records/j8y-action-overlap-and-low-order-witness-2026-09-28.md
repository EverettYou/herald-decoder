## Summary

Two preregistered cheap reductions fail on the completed 12-sector exact class. Pairwise action/error overlap is not enough to group sectors with the same complete second-law contrast: two repeated geometry signatures each contain different exact total variations. In one positive five-edge-action cell, the full second public record has total variation `3/4` between the two private errors, while every one-charge-bit and every two-charge-bit marginal has total variation zero. The information in that cell is necessarily a higher-order joint charge correlation, not a locally readable single or pair bit.

## Evidence

The [prospective two-branch contract](../../manifests/j8y-action-overlap-and-low-order-witness-2026-09-28.json) freezes all 24 sector/action cells in the [direct public-law census](../../results/j8x-deterministic-first-collision-census-2026-09-28.json), tests both support-overlap geometry and every one-/two-charge-bit marginal, and prohibits new Born evaluations. The [exact diagnostic result](../../results/j8y-action-overlap-and-low-order-witness-2026-09-28.json) replays all 24 full-law TVs and 7,200 marginal comparisons; data-processing inequalities pass. Six cells have positive full TV; four exceed their best one-bit marginal and one exceeds every two-bit marginal. The latter is first-flux mask `3591` under the fixed five-edge action: full TV `3/4`, maximum one-bit TV `0`, maximum two-bit TV `0`. Two repeated action-overlap signatures contain mixed zero/nonzero full TVs, so overlap counts cannot justify pooling. Source hashes and zero new Born/history/arm/bootstrap gates pass in `0.45304` CPU seconds.

## Status

The registered diagnostic is closed. This is a finite restricted public-record law result; it does not identify the minimum sufficient joint order beyond two, establish a reusable operator automorphism, measure other records or sectors, or support full-IID information, logical-risk, noisy-JIT or threshold claims. A next bounded exact contraction may locate the minimum joint charge order in the fixed witness; any broader law reuse still needs independent direct validation.
