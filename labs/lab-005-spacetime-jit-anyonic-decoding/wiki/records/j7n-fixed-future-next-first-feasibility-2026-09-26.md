## Summary

Under either of two held-fixed later single-edge faults, the original
two corrections continue to have identical complete next public flux.
Each action/fault cell has one cross-round anticommuting operator pair,
but the current exact full-law engine would exceed its existing term
ceiling already in the one-site screening stage. No next-first Born law
was evaluated.

## Evidence

The [prospective four-cell contract](../../manifests/j7n-fixed-future-next-first-feasibility-2026-09-26.json)
crosses the two public actions and all 34 positive complete second
prefixes from the [preceding exact law](j7m-five-site-complete-second-charge-law-2026-09-26.md)
with the two earlier frozen later-fault keys `[0]` and `[4]`. It
requires matching next flux, sector eligibility, a bounded
second/next operator-commutator screen, and an exact *work projection*
from the existing algorithm's registered bit-count rule. Private
physical error, fault, final support and operators remain diagnostic
internals rather than decoder-visible information.

The [machine result](../../results/j7n-fixed-future-next-first-feasibility-2026-09-26.json)
passes eight immutable pins and all four action/future cell checks.
Each cell has 22 eligible next-star sites and exactly one cross-round
anticommuting second/next pair. The previously computed complete
second prefixes number two under baseline and 32 under the loop
action, with no prefix selection or dropped outcome. The unchanged
exact implementation's denominator-plus-one-site screen would use
5,696 terms per baseline cell and 23,085,056 per loop cell, totaling
46,181,504 across both future keys. That is well above the previous
65,536-term ceiling *before* a complete next joint law. The
calculation checked 1,936 operator pairs but evaluated zero Born
moments, next laws, stochastic histories, schedule arms or bootstraps.

## Status

The anticommutator is only a necessary structural opening for some
cross-round effects; it does not establish a nonzero next-public
contrast. The work count characterizes this implementation, not a
fundamental lower bound. A separate prospectively bounded analytical
reduction around the single witness may be affordable; silently
expanding the old exact budget or reporting a next-law effect is not.
Policy emission, an error ensemble and paired logical-risk scoring
also remain unestablished.
