## Summary

At the frozen ideal L=2, 36-edge IID red-X rate `p_X=1/10`, the uncomputed private-error contribution to the posterior after the selected all-zero-charge complete first record is at most `0.005032`. For each of the three other tested complete first records, the corresponding worst-case bound is about `0.1703`. These are rigorous upper bounds for four records within one first-flux sector, not measured omitted posterior masses or a full-IID information result.

## Evidence

The [prospective four-record contract](../../manifests/j8l-first-record-tail-certificate-2026-09-27.json) pins the corrected first-public interface, the two fixed relation-free actions, exact rational prior, known-law sets and 106-cell/60-CPU-second ceilings. Its first invocation failed closed before writing a result due to a runner manifest-key path; the only amendment repaired that path and re-pinned the runner.

The [verified machine result](../../results/j8l-first-record-tail-certificate-2026-09-27.json) combines the [selected-flux prior mass](../../results/j8j-full-prior-coverage-feasibility-verified-2026-09-27.json), the [corrected four-record first masses](../../results/j8k-corrected-alternate-first-record-masses-2026-09-27.json), and the [67-support selected-record law set](../../results/j8i-all-support-complete-second-laws-verified-2026-09-27.json). For each record `o`, exact known joint mass is `A=Σ_known P(e)P(o|e)` and omitted sector prior mass is `T=P(selected flux)−Σ_known P(e)`. Because every omitted likelihood is at most one, its posterior weight cannot exceed `T/(A+T)`. The all-zero-charge record uses 67 known supports and gives `0.005031330054`; charges at site 1 or 2 each use 13 known supports and give `0.169854320857`; charges at both sites use 13 and give `0.170289281145`. The known positive subsets have equal complete second public laws under both fixed actions. All 106 reused error/record cells, exact prior-weight replay, public-vacuum checks, input hashes, and zero-new-Born-term budget pass.

## Status

The all-zero-charge selected-record mixture is now tightly *bounded* for bounded observables whose known-subset laws coincide, but its unknown tail was not evaluated. The other three bounds remain too loose for a strong null. No conclusion follows for other first-charge records, other first-flux sectors, full-channel information, unconditional logical risk, noisy scheduling, larger sizes or threshold. The next independent bounded prerequisite should test whether a symmetry or likelihood constraint can tighten the alternate-record tail under the inherited budget; a full-channel claim additionally requires other flux sectors.
