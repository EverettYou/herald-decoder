## Summary

The existing exact projector algorithm cannot complete a single all-54-support second-public-law matrix under its inherited 65,536-orbit-term cap. A prospectively registered cost preflight closed 40 of the 51 previously untested L=2 supports under both fixed actions, then stopped at the first singleton orbit-term cap. It did not evaluate any complete second public law or establish a null for the remaining support class.

## Evidence

The [prospective contract](../../manifests/j8e-remaining-support-second-law-cost-2026-09-27.json) freezes every remaining eight-edge same-first-flux support and the three earlier representatives as replay controls, with both actions, the selected complete first record, and unchanged ideal L=2 orbit. It permits exact one-site second-charge expectations and relation-aware structural cost projections, but no higher-order conditional probabilities or complete laws. It caps actual orbit work at 65,536 terms, relation probes at 200,000 and CPU at 60 seconds.

The [machine result](../../results/j8e-remaining-support-second-law-cost-2026-09-27.json) closes 40 candidate rows and 80 action cells: 13 in winding `(1,-1)`, 11 in `(1,0)` and 16 in `(0,1)`. Every completed cell has 22 eligible second sites and either one or five variable charge bits. The stopped run includes 18,464 first and 46,080 singleton second orbit moments (64,544 actual), and 194,507 actual-plus-projected relation probes. Its recorded relation-aware orbit projection is 187,936 terms on the processed prefix, including the next candidate's completed first action and partly processed second action, already above the inherited 65,536 cap. Neither action of that candidate is released as a completed paired row among the 80 cells. The stop gate is `singleton_orbit_term_cap`. All input hashes passed before and after. The other 11 candidate rows and all three replay controls were not reached. No full law, history, schedule arm or bootstrap ran.

## Status

This is a cost-censored prerequisite, not a scientific second-law result. The projection rules out an all-support run *with this exact expansion algorithm and single inherited cap*; it is not a lower bound for every possible algebraic or symmetry-reduced method. Completed singleton checks do not imply complete-law equality. The next bounded diagnostic is to test whether source-validated operator/symmetry reuse can remove redundant orbit expansions while preserving all support and action branches, or else register fixed nonselective batches only if their combined cost stays within the already approved compute budget. Do not ask a human merely to rank the three winding classes. Full-IID information, logical risk, noisy JIT, L=3 laws and thresholds remain open.
