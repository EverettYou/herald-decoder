## Summary

The smallest structurally open two-edge history comparison is exactly null
for the complete public next-first record. At each of the two matched first
records, total variation between the two branch laws is zero after each
branch's own second outcome is marginalized. This is a finite ideal-instrument
negative control, not evidence that general D4 measurements forget history.

## Status

Closed as a two-edge exact null despite a verified cross-round operator
anticommutator. Noncommutation was a necessary structural opening but not a
sufficient public-law effect here. Because the histories use different
second-measurement sectors and future-fault timing, this does not isolate a
correction effect. No general stateful D4 physical channel, noisy JIT
advantage, scaling or threshold follows. The next bounded scientific step
must reassess whether additional ideal exact fixtures have discriminating
power before spending compute on a larger matrix or caller integration.
