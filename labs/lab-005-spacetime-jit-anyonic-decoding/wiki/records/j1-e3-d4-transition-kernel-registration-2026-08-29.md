# J1 E3 repeated D4 transition-kernel audit registration

The next Lab 005 gate is not a noisy schedule benchmark. It first asks whether the released spatial D4 sampler, repeated-projector instrument, action-bound E2 interface, and private homology scorer jointly specify every factor of a normalized causal law

\[
P(X_{t+1},O_{t+1}\mid X_t,F_t,A_t).
\]

The primary-source boundary makes this audit necessary. Jing 2025 supplies a localized time-like-herald example rather than a complete repeated multisite channel. Jing 2026 defines spacetime error and fusion-channel variables but leaves numerical implementation and benchmarking open. Lyons and Brown supply a local noisy D(S3)/D(Z3) construction, not a D4 transfer law. The project therefore may not manufacture a memoryless D4 history by independently resampling the existing single-round fusion record.

The registered K1-K4 matrix separately audits physical-state evolution, the public measurement kernel, the action-conditioned post-flux kernel, and the private ground-state-relative logical scorer. Each factor must have an operational input/output law, normalization or a deterministic identity, causal ownership, and an explicit source-versus-project label. Exact paper-\(L=2\) limiting cases are allowed; Monte Carlo and performance sampling are not.

If every factor is complete, the next contract may implement the smallest generator. If a factor is missing, the audit will localize it before any modeling choice or human escalation. A researcher decision is needed only if the sources and bounded exact checks leave multiple materially different physical laws unresolved.
