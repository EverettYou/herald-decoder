## B11--B18 presentation corrections

B11 reanalysed the cohort. B12 separates evidence layers instead of imposing one categorical map.

![B12 three-layer evidence map.](../figures/phase-b12-honeycomb-three-layer-phase-map-2026-08-28.png)

B13 promotes a continuous log-odds trend field.

![B13 continuous log-odds field.](../figures/phase-b13-honeycomb-continuous-log-odds-map-2026-08-28.png)

B14 acquired lower-transition leverage without a standalone raster. B15 compares flexible visual summaries and motivates stronger regularization.

![B15 neural versus spline boundary comparison.](../figures/phase-b15-neural-spline-boundary-comparison-2026-08-28.png)

B16 attempted a strongly smoothed guide but is withdrawn from active
presentation. Its unconstrained controls create an unsupported low-q
right-then-left reversal, and its conditional 64-refit envelope is much
narrower than the finite-grid/model uncertainty.

![B16 strongly smoothed finite-window guide.](../figures/phase-b16-strong-smooth-boundary-2026-08-28.png)

B17 corrected the reversal and uncertainty display. It keeps the dashed curve only
as a monotone visual guide and replaces the narrow B16 envelope with the
conservative nondecreasing region containing sixteen B11 directional brackets
and three B14 high-q right-censoring brackets. The region is not a confidence
set for a physical boundary. The figure contains no curve legend or fitting
method footer.

![B17 resolution-aware finite-window guide.](../figures/phase-b17-resolution-aware-guide-2026-08-28.png)

B18 is the active collaborator-facing summary. It retains the B17 evidence
region but enforces the physical q_c(p)=q_c(1-p) symmetry through an exact
horizontal endpoint tangent at p=0.5. The p axis now shows the full [0,0.5]
fundamental domain; the unsampled low-p strip is not extrapolated.

![B18 symmetry-constrained finite-window guide.](../figures/phase-b18-symmetry-constrained-guide-2026-08-28.png)
