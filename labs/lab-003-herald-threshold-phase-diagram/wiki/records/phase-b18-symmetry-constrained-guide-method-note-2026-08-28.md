# Withdrawn B18 guide

## Summary

The guide is withdrawn; the reason and replacement are stated below.

## Status

Withdrawn scientific interpretation; historical provenance only.

## Evidence

B18 imposed p↔1−p symmetry and a horizontal boundary tangent at half for the incomplete binary-herald model. These assumptions are incorrect for interior q and the guide is withdrawn. Original trials are preserved; the fitted guide is not evidence or an answer key.

The physical domain is p,q in [0,1]. The current B19 figure displays measured coverage without a boundary curve; high-p joint-grid values are not inferred by reflection. See [corrected model, endpoint exceptions and exact counterexample](/wiki?page=methods/binary-herald-full-prior-domain.md) and [current evidence](/lab?id=lab-003-herald-threshold-phase-diagram).
