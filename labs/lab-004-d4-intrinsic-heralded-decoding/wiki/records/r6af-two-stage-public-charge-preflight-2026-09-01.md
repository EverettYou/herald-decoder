# R6AF two-stage public-charge preflight

Date: 2026-09-01  
Status: **passed after public-interface remediation; pilot not yet registered**

## Outcome

The source-audited two-stage gate was registered, failed closed once, then was
remediated and rerun on the identical paper-normalized $L=2$ fixture. No
production trajectories were allocated.

The causal structure passed: O0 and O2 consume the same physical error and
the same corrected signal-only first record, choose different first
corrections on the discriminating fixture, and then generate their second
records from different action-conditioned supports using the same exogenous
random key. Each internal second record satisfies its own parity support. The
zero-error fixture decodes without loss, and the first-stage terminal decision
uses Boolean physical/correction-union homology.

## Public-interface remediation

The initial run localized two failures: inactive `-1` sentinels appeared in
the stored second record, and `recover_postflux_charges` accepted the hidden
relation graph at the combined action/scoring boundary. The replacement
interface now:

1. projects simulator outcomes to a full binary decoder-visible record with
   zero outside hidden active support;
2. computes blue and green MWPM actions through
   `decode_public_postflux_charges(honeycomb, public_charge_outcomes)`, whose
   signature contains no relation or active-support input;
3. supplies hidden relations only to a separate support validator and logical
   scorer; and
4. exports an explicit public transcript without physical errors, active
   support, relation partitions, effective errors, or logical truth.

The wrong-action binding fixture also passes fail-closed behavior: an O0
second record containing a positive value outside the O2 action-conditioned
support is rejected when scored against the O2 hidden branch.

The machine result localizes both failures in
[`r6af-two-stage-public-charge-preflight-2026-09-01.json`](../../results/r6af-two-stage-public-charge-preflight-2026-09-01.json).
All R6AF gates pass, and 15 focused charge, pipeline, anti-leak, and
action-binding tests pass.

## Claim boundary and next gate

This preflight releases no logical-error rate, crossing, threshold, or
cross-lab comparison. It authorizes registration—not execution—of the
smallest paired O0/O2 two-stage pilot. The pilot grid, paired uncertainty,
budget, and stop rule must be frozen in the manifest before histories are
allocated.
