## Synthesis

R6AF agrees qualitatively with the low/intermediate-prior R4.5c public direction, and both studies independently identify first-stage policy choice as a major lever. The R4.5c p=0.6 public-fixed regression prevents a universal O2-dominance claim.

The agreement is qualitative only. R4.5c and R6AF differ in topology, size, conditioning, p grid, charge-graph representation, and exact-versus-Monte-Carlo evidence. Their risk and delta magnitudes are not compared.

## Nonuniversal boundary

R4.5c public fixed O2 is slightly worse than public fixed O0 at p=0.6 even though its future-aware exact O2 risk remains lower. Any synthesis must preserve this counterexample and avoid claiming O2 dominance for all priors or sizes.
