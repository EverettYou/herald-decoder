## Size direction and stage mechanism

The independent complete-trajectory bootstrap for `risk(L=3)-risk(L=2)` gives:

- heralded: decreasing risk at `p=0.14,0.16,0.18,0.20`; unresolved at `p=0.22`;
- syndrome-only: decreasing at `p=0.14`, unresolved at `p=0.16`, and increasing
  at `p=0.18,0.20,0.22`.

These are two-size directions, not crossings or threshold estimates. The stage
counts localize the policy difference primarily to the first Boolean-union
winding decision. For example, at `L=3,p=0.20`, syndrome-only has 953
first-stage failures versus 492 for heralded; final risks are 0.5260 and
0.2825. Across all cells, terminal physical-winding histories are zero, so the
registered decoder-contingent sensitivity is numerically identical to the full
paired comparison.
