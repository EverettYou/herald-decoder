# R4.2 complete distinct-observation matrix — registered method

## Question and claim boundary

R4.2 asks how exact conditioned logical-sector inference behaves over every
physically supported observation on the primitive 12-edge D4 fixture, rather
than on the two hand-selected R4.0/R4.1 observations. The strongest allowed
claim is a complete exact audit of this bounded fixture. It is not an LER
experiment, a threshold result, or evidence that the exact algorithm scales.

This is the intrinsic-information layer of the program: it measures the Bayes
limit for the declared local fusion record. It does not compare Lab 002 or the
published practical decoder yet.

## Complete observation construction

Enumerate all 4096 physical edge masks. Any mask with nontrivial winding is a
terminal ground-state-relative logical failure under resolved Discussion
thread 008. Such masks remain outside the conditioned posterior and receive no
invented charge record.

For each nonwinding mask, enumerate every charge assignment on exactly its
degree-two internal vertices that satisfies the geometry-derived parity
constraints. Each allowed record has the already validated Appendix-A weight

\[
P(s\mid E)=2^{C-N_{\mathrm{internal}}}.
\]

The full flux vector and full `{-1,0,1}` charge vector form the observation key.
Identical keys are deduplicated while preserving every compatible error mask.
The candidate-observation incidence table is guarded at one million pairs.

## Prior matrix and posterior quantities

Use `p={0.1,0.3,0.5,0.6}`. The endpoints preserve the existing exact semantic
fixtures, `p=0.3` probes the sparse-error interior, and `p=0.5` removes the
Bernoulli weight preference so only the conditioned observation likelihood
distinguishes configurations.

For every distinct observation and prior, sum `P(s|E)P(E)` in relative logical
sectors. Report compatible-candidate count, sector count, conditional entropy,
Bayes failure probability, and the top-two sector evidence gap. Summaries must
include both structural counts over distinct observations and operational
distributions weighted by observation probability, normalized separately over
the nonterminal mass.

Configuration MAP is treated set-wise because tied configurations may occupy
different sectors. Compare the sectors containing every maximum-weight
configuration with the maximum-evidence sector set and report singleton
agreement, overlapping ambiguity, and forced disjoint disagreement. A
lexicographic configuration-MAP convention may be reported only as a separate
deterministic implementation diagnostic.

## Validation and stopping

The audit must classify all 4096 masks exactly once, reproduce the 123 R3.5
terminal winding masks, normalize allowed records for every nonwinding mask,
and verify that terminal prior mass plus all nonwinding observation evidence is
one for each prior. Sector posteriors must normalize; the exact R4.0 fractions
and R4.1 reference/summation fixtures must remain unchanged; and an independent
observation subset must agree with the existing exact posterior routine.

Stop after the primitive exact matrix, tests, JSON audit, Markdown report, and
Lab report update. Do not enlarge the lattice or launch stochastic/LER samples.
