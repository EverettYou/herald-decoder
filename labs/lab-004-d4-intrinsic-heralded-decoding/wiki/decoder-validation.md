## Status

Current validated spatial-pipeline boundary. The R6AF public-interface gate
passes, and a four-cell paired O0/O2 implementation pilot is registered at
$L=3,5$ and $p_X=0.19,0.21$ with 512 histories per cell. The completed
[pilot](records/r6af-two-stage-public-charge-pilot-2026-09-01.md) resolves
lower O2 final loss in all four cells under pointwise paired intervals, with
the difference dominated by first-stage Boolean-union failures. It is not a
crossing, threshold, scaling, noisy-measurement, or fault-tolerance study.

The completed [R6AG synthesis](records/r6ag-r4-r6af-source-consistency-synthesis-2026-09-01.md)
finds qualitative consistency between R6AF and exact primitive R4.5c at the
level of public O0/O2 direction and first-stage leverage. It preserves the
R4.5c $p=0.6$ public-fixed O2 regression as a nonuniversal boundary. Numeric
risks and deltas remain incommensurate because topology, size, conditioning,
parameter grid, charge graph, and exact-versus-Monte-Carlo evidence differ.

The completed [R6AH provenance audit](records/r6ah-r5a-r6af-public-interface-provenance-audit-2026-09-01.md)
finds legacy R5a incompatible with the remediated complete public interface.
All 40,000 records expose inactive support in the first record; every stored
second record retains sentinels; and the old charge boundary mixes public
action with private relation/effective-error truth. R5a physical and lattice
records remain provenance, but its numerical complete-pipeline claims and Lab
005 baseline promotion are withdrawn.

The prospective
[R6AJ contract](../manifests/r6aj-corrected-public-interface-transfer-manifest-2026-09-01.json)
is the only active replacement for that transfer question. It freezes the
remediated signal-only first record, action-conditioned full-binary second
record, relation-free charge action, private generation/scoring boundary,
matched keys, unconditional Boolean-union denominator, a bounded
$L=7,9$ by $p_X=0.17,0.21$ grid, paired uncertainty, hard compute cap,
and fail-closed anti-leak/action-binding/replay gates. Registration produced
zero histories, arm evaluations, and bootstrap replicates. The superseded R6AI
crossing smoke contains 24 excluded provenance records and cannot support a
plot, fit, or scientific claim.
