# Inherited evidence and the theory question

## Summary

Explain the group, geometry, and orientation contrasts of Lab 006 by deriving
the decoding problem's statistical-mechanics model and separating its intrinsic
posterior structure from practical hard decoding. The inherited curves motivate the theory; new results are owned by the
partition-function, conjugacy, statistical-model, and decoder-gap pages.

## Evidence

The parent [final interpretation](../../lab-006-sun-bp-theory/wiki/interpretation.md)
and [paired analysis](../../lab-006-sun-bp-theory/results/a9-orientation-analysis.json)
provide 600 audited cells and 12,000,000 paired trials. All 4,000,000 SU(2)
trials agree between orientation channels. The 313 significant U(1)/SU(3)
changes all increase LER; their direction, power, and finite-size limits are
owned by the parent's [orientation evidence](../../lab-006-sun-bp-theory/wiki/hidden-orientation.md).

The leading clue is self-conjugacy of SU(2)'s pseudoreal fundamental, compared
with inequivalent U(1) charges and SU(3) fundamental/antifundamental. The inherited
local quotient is established for the parent's classical fusion law; its ability
to explain the observed size curves remains open. Square hidden-U(1) curve
merging is a researcher-suggested KT/BKT-like hypothesis, with ordinary
finite-size drift and decoder effects retained as alternatives.

The parent [method](../../../wiki/methods/sun-fusion-herald-belief-propagation.md)
defines “hardening”: posterior activity beliefs become log-odds matching weights
and then a syndrome-compatible correction. It does not independently round edge
beliefs. Exact logical-sector inference, exact-marginal MWPM, and BP-MWPM are
three distinct targets for the new theory.
