# Shared Wiki

The Wiki is the project’s compiled knowledge layer. It has one project-wide scope: references contribute cited background; completed lab reports contribute evidence-backed findings. It is not a duplicate archive of references or lab reports.

## Ownership

- `concepts/`: scientific vocabulary and established background.
- `methods/`: decoding, modeling, and analysis methods.
- `references/`: compact routers to paper, repository, dataset, and other reference records.
- `labs/`: compact routers to completed lab evidence.

Each promoted claim must cite its source path or canonical URL and distinguish evidence, synthesis, and open questions.
